# Battle benchmark

One recording path measures a real simulation and renderer while a fixed camera tour flies. A preset names the work; it is never a fallback for a missing map or a failed preparation.

The default village control keeps its supported blue comparison script, defender and historical contact tick. The named `city-contact` selection uses the existing simulation-owned central contact stress on a complete generated world. Its synthetic starts and seeded orders are explicit; it does not measure player deployment or replace infantry travel. The map enters through the normal map source and preparation worker, and that worker becomes the battle authority.

A report records the selected workload fingerprint and exact applied tour. Generated reports also retain the checked request, returned map identity, derived map extents and stress identity. The fight stays at the playable centre; the overview frames the rendered landscape, including its visual-only margin. The existing display environment is drawn and counted too. Compare rows only when those inputs agree; a generator, physical catalogue, margin or rule change creates a different control even if the preset name stays the same.

Leaving preparation closes its worker. Once ready, timing starts after the declared warm tick and the first drawn frame; a cancellation produces a partial result rather than a complete performance verdict. Both presets use the same short and full windows and scene owner. Selecting and preparing a distinct workload is a functional proof; the full timed window remains the frame-cost gate.
