# Spike 03 verdict: rig, clips, tank articulation

**Verdict: reslice.** The technique is a go, but the look kill criterion tripped.

- **Rig, clips and articulation: go.**
  - One CC0 skeleton carries a Blender-scripted modern rifleman and all six clip roles. Three roles are library clips with an authored rifle-hold arm layer; two are fully authored.
  - The rig holds a two-handed rifle, low ready and shouldered, with both hands on the weapon.
  - A scripted tank and supply truck carry every named moving part, and they articulate on the simulation's own geometry.
  - The licences are accepted (`decisions.md`, 2026-09-25).
- **Look: the kill criterion tripped.** Two unprimed critiques, one before and one after a fix pass, called the soldier and the tank "toy-like". They called the truck "placeholder" in round 2 and "placeholder-plus, bordering on acceptable" in round 1. Details are under "Critique".
  - The defects are modelling effort: kit that floats or clips, brick boots, inflated clothing shells, a plain primitive truck, and no baked wear. Prone and death also need rework.
  - None is a rig limit. The contract's fallback, "another CC0 rig", would not fix any of them, so it is not taken.
  - The critique rates the tank closest to acceptable: "the massing and articulation are right".

**What the user must decide** (not chosen silently):
- **(a)** Slices 21 and 22 get a real modelling budget, with the critique gate kept. That means a hand-modelled uniform, boots and plate carrier on the UBC body, a detailed hull, and a baked wear and grime pass. This is the recommended option.
- **(b)** A deliberately stylised soldier.
- **(c)** Also, independent of (a) or (b): the `tank_muzzle_local_m` rule retune (see the tank section).

All numbers were measured 2026-09-25 with Blender 5.2.1 LTS, headless (`Blender -b --python`), with no MCP server. Everything was built in `throwaway/spike03/` (gitignored) and never merges.

## The rig

One skin with **65 joints**, identical by name and hierarchy across UAL1, UAL2 and UBC (UE5-mannequin style). Every UAL clip plays on the UBC body unchanged.

```
root
└ pelvis
  ├ spine_01 ─ spine_02 ─ spine_03
  │   ├ neck_01 ─ Head
  │   ├ clavicle_l ─ upperarm_l ─ lowerarm_l ─ hand_l ─ {index,middle,ring,pinky,thumb}_{01,02,03,04_leaf}_l
  │   └ clavicle_r ─ … mirror … ─ hand_r ─ …_r
  ├ thigh_l ─ calf_l ─ foot_l ─ ball_l ─ ball_leaf_l
  └ thigh_r ─ calf_r ─ foot_r ─ ball_r ─ ball_leaf_r
```

- 5 + 2 + 2×(4+20) + 2×5 = 65, with 40 of them finger joints. The bundle could drop the `*_leaf` joints (10), which carry no weights, to get 55.
- **Weights:** at most 4 influences on every vertex (1,057 verts at 1, 4,335 at 2, 876 at 3, 1,013 at 4), so the validator's ≤4 rule holds without a reweight.
- **Basis:** in Blender after glTF import, the rig is Z up, faces −Y, and its left is +X. A **+90° rotation about Z** on the root gives the engine basis (Z up, +X forward). Measured after conversion: toe direction (0.95, −0.10, −0.31) in idle, (0.98, 0.12, −0.16) kneeling. The origin is at the ground between the feet, and minZ is −0.0095 m on the source body, inside ±1 cm.
- **Scale:** the UBC male is 1.81 m. It is scaled by 1.70 / 1.81 = 0.939 to `physics.soldier_height_m`. The eye then sits at 1.53 m in idle, against `infantry_eye_m` 1.6.

## Clip coverage

| role | source | how |
|---|---|---|
| idle | library `Idle_Loop` | Legs and torso from the library. Arms come from IK to a rifle held at low ready, parented to `spine_03`, plus a gripping-finger pose. Baked to FK. |
| walk | library `Walk_Loop` | as idle |
| run | library `Jog_Fwd_Loop` | As idle, with spine and neck twist damped to 45% so the rifle stays forward. |
| kneel_fire | **authored** | Right knee down, left foot forward (leg IK), blade stance, cheek weld (head and neck tilt), rifle shouldered. A recoil kick fires at frame 3 and peaks at frame 5: 4 cm back and 5° up, measured as muzzle z 1.045 to 1.092. It is a 36-frame loop. |
| prone_pinned | **authored** | Pelvis rotated 90° face down, body on the ground, feet toes-down. Rifle across in front of the head, head tucked with a flinch at frames 24–30. It is a 60-frame loop. |
| death | library `Death01` | Body from the library; arms keep the low-ready IK hold, so the rifle falls with him across the chest. Raw `Death01` flings the arms up and held the rifle aloft; the first critique caught it. |
| corpse (static) | library `Death01` last frame | |

The library has no rifle-hold, rifle locomotion, kneel-fire or prone clip in either free tier, which confirms `decisions.md`. UAL1's 43 clips are A_TPose, Crouch_Fwd/Idle, Dance, Death01, Driving, Fixing_Kneeling, Hit_Chest/Head, Idle(+Talking, Torch), Interact, Jog_Fwd, Jump_*, PickUp_Table, Pistol_{Aim_*, Idle, Reload, Shoot}, Punch_*, Push, Roll, Sitting_*, Spell_Simple_*, Sprint, Swim_*, Sword_Attack/Idle and Walk/Walk_Formal. UAL2 adds fantasy, melee, farm and zombie clips, plus `LayToIdle`, `Hit_Knockback`, `Walk_Carry` and `ClimbUp_1m`. None of UAL2 is needed for the six roles.

**The authoring method** (the proof of the missing clips) is a scripted constraint rig baked to FK:
1. Each hold is a rifle placement, as a matrix. The right-hand IK target sits at the pistol grip and the left at the handguard, both defined in the rifle's own frame. Hand orientation comes from a fingers direction and a palm normal. There are elbow pole targets.
2. Legs for kneel get their own IK targets, and torso poses are set by armature-space rotations.
3. `bpy.ops.nla.bake(visual_keying=True)` writes a plain FK action.

Because every hold defines the right hand in the rifle's frame, the rifle is **rigid on `hand_r` in every clip**. Slice 21 can therefore skin the weapon to `hand_r` at bake time, with no attachment system. Measured wrist-to-handguard residuals are 0.095–0.14 m. That is the wrist-to-palm offset, so the palm is on the handguard. Prone is 0.18: the left hand is short of the handguard.

## The rifleman (Blender-scripted kit on the UBC body)

- **Uniform, boots, gloves, vest and belt** are *shells cut from the body*. Each copies the body, keeps a region by height and weight, merges the glTF seam duplicates, smooths away the anatomy, pushes out along the normals and solidifies. Each keeps every vertex group, so it skins with the body for free. The body is then cut back to head and neck.
- Helmet (ear-cut dome, NVG mount, rails), front and back plates, pouches, pack, radio and antenna are bone-parented to `Head`, `spine_03`, `calf_*` and `pelvis`.
- The rifle is a modern carbine from about 14 parts, 0.86 m, with `rifle_muzzle`, `rifle_support` and `rifle_butt` sockets.
- **Triangles:** 28,348 at LOD0, or 26,596 without the eye and eyebrow meshes. By piece: uniform 10,060, gloves 6,728 (finger shells, the cheapest win), head 2,960, vest 2,156, boots about 1.1k (shaft shell plus rigid boot and sole on `foot_*`), kit and rifle about 2k. A naive Decimate before the armature gives 9,878 / 4,204 / 1,718 at 0.4 / 0.15 / 0.05. Only the counts were measured, not the look.
- **Glb (glTF float export, all six clips):** 3,504,084 bytes. Accessors are 1,580,184, of which animation is 111,824 (the exporter drops constant channels). The rest is the UBC eye and hair PNGs, which slice 21 drops. The source `UAL1_Standard.glb` carries 5.15 MB of float animation for its 43 clips.

## The tank: `turret`, `gun`, `muzzle`, `hmg`, road wheels, `track_L/R`

```
tank ─ hull ─┬ turret (yaw about the hull origin, z 1.45) ─┬ gun (pitch at the trunnion (1.70, 0, 2.00)) ─ muzzle
             │                                            └ hmg (yaw on the commander's cupola) ─ hmg_gun (pitch) ─ hmg_muzzle
             ├ wheel_{L,R}_{1..7}, wheel_{L,R}_{sprocket,idler,return_1,return_2}   (spin about +Y by distance / radius)
             └ track_L, track_R   (a closed band mesh; U = arc length / 0.16 m link pitch; material scrolls by the track_offset property)
```

- **Muzzle arc check:** turret yaw alone moves `muzzle` exactly along the simulation's `position + rotate([r, 0], bearing).with_z(2)`. The worst error over 12 bearings is 1.1e-6 m. The turret axis is the hull origin, as in `weapons.rs::muzzle`.
- **Articulation strip:** 8 phases of turret yaw 0–315°, gun pitch −8° to +20°, and independent HMG yaw and pitch. The HMG turns independently of the turret, as in the decision "two turret mounts, cannon and HMG, each with its own published pose".
- **Running gear strip:** 8 phases of 0.1 m of travel. The wheel holes rotate and the track links scroll in step. Wheel radius 0.33 m; track loop 14.65 m, from the convex hull of all wheels.
- **Triangles:** 31,828 in all. The hull with skirts is 12,368, turret 2,200, gun 816, HMG 428, and each wheel 716 (22 wheels are about 15.8k, so wheels are the LOD budget). Glb 997,228 bytes.
- **Bounds against `tank_half_extents_m` [3.5, 1.8, 1.2]:** the hull is −3.575 to +3.5 in x, the skirts ±1.83 in y, the turret roof at 2.15 m, the cupola and HMG at 2.79 m, and the gun reaches x = 5.9.
- **Landmine: `tank_muzzle_local_m` [3, 0, 2] makes a stub gun.** With the turret axis at the hull origin, a 3.0 m reach puts the muzzle 0.5 m *inside* the hull front. It renders as a stub (`throwaway/spike03/out/tank_reach_compare.png`, right). A real MBT's muzzle sits about 5.8–5.9 m ahead of the hull centre: Abrams is 7.93 m of hull and 9.77 m overall. Slice 22 needs the user to choose:
  - retune the rule to about [5.9, 0, 2.0]. That moves the fire origin 2.9 m and changes digests, so it is a named decision;
  - or keep the rule and let the validator's muzzle check tolerate a presentation muzzle that differs from the sim's.

  Recommended: retune the rule.

## The supply truck: `wheel_*`, `deploy_leg_*`, `deploy_mast`

```
truck ─ body ─┬ wheel_{F,M,R}{L,R}  (radius 0.55)
              ├ deploy_leg_{FL,FR,RL,RR} (outrigger beam slides out 0.55 m) ─ deploy_leg_*_jack ─ deploy_leg_*_pad (lowers to the ground)
              └ deploy_mast (hinge: lies along the roof, raises to vertical) ─ deploy_mast_2 ─ deploy_mast_3 ─ deploy_mast_head
```

One function drives it: `set_deploy(progress)`, which is `DeploymentState.progress`. It runs in overlapping phases:

| phase | progress |
|---|---|
| beams out | 0–0.3 |
| jacks down | 0.2–0.5 |
| mast up | 0.35–0.65 |
| telescope | 0.6–1.0 |

Measured: the pads sit at z 0.60 stowed and 0.025 deployed, on the ground. The mast head reaches 9.3 m. The truck is 7,972 triangles and the glb 362,120 bytes. It is a 6×6 with a cab-forward cab and a shelter body, sized to `supply_half_extents_m` [3, 1.4, 1.8].

## Critique

Two fresh sub-agents critiqued the full sheets and strips, with crops of `defilade/x-saving-wounded-f4.jpg` (the two soldiers by the wall) and `warno/steam-warno-1.jpg` (the nearest tank). They had no project context.

**Round 1, on the first pass: soldier toy-like, tank toy-like, truck "placeholder-plus".** It found:
- sock-like boots;
- the death clip holding the rifle aloft;
- an oversized box turret and hidden road wheels;
- the stub 3 m gun: the long gun "reads far more like a real modern tank";
- a gap between mast segments.

All of these were fixed. Death now keeps the rifle hold. The turret is smaller, lower and has sloped sides, and the skirts are shorter. Boots are rigid on `foot_*`. The mast segment overlap is fixed.

**Round 2, after the fixes: soldier toy-like, tank toy-like ("the closest to acceptable": massing and articulation right), truck placeholder.** Its remaining findings:
- **Soldier:**
  - brick boots with sole plates that stick out;
  - knee pads and leg pouches floating off the leg;
  - the plate as a separate slab, with the forearm passing through it while running;
  - inflated limbs and bodybuilder shoulders;
  - bald scalp below the helmet rim;
  - prone reading as broken: a face crater between the arms, the antenna through the pack, no hand on the rifle in one view, and nearly static;
  - death jumping from crouched to flat between the 8 sampled phases;
  - idle nearly static;
  - hands off the handguard in walk and run at some phases.
- **Tank:**
  - smoke launchers floating;
  - bare idlers and a wheel-to-track gap;
  - a flat ribbon track;
  - no wear;
  - tow hooks floating.
- **Truck:** a blockout form, and a deploy it found incoherent.
- **Where it agreed with the spike:** kneel_fire is the best pose ("two hands on the rifle, cheek at the optic"). The soldier's battle-distance silhouette reads clearly, "cleaner than the blurry Defilade crop".

Checked against the scripts, three findings are wrong:
- Gun pitch never exceeds +15°. The "60–70°" is the 40° camera pitch.
- The tan tube on the truck roof is the tarp roll, not a second mast.
- The idle and kneel strips move little by design: the library idle, and a 4 cm recoil.

Confirmed by the measurements:
- the left hand is short of the handguard in prone, by 0.18 m;
- the run arm swing still pulls hands off the handguard at some phases, from the damped library torso.

These go on slice 21's and 22's fix lists. The kill criterion is recorded as tripped on look, not on technique.

## What the consuming slices must do

**Slice 11 (bundle format):**
- The skeleton is the 65-joint UBC/UAL rig, with skeleton id e.g. `quaternius-ubc-v2`. Clips are shared per skeleton, and one clip set serves every infantry kind.
- Clips are small: six roles are about 112 KB as float glTF after constant-channel pruning. Quantised quaternions shrink them further, so the "shared once per skeleton" encoding is cheap.
- Basis conversion is a +90° Z root rotation from the glTF-imported rig. Put it in the bake, not the loader.
- Required articulated nodes are confirmed as named above. Add these to the validator's list:
  - `hmg_gun` and `hmg_muzzle` (the HMG's pitch node and muzzle);
  - the `deploy_leg_*_jack` / `_pad` and `deploy_mast_2/3/head` children;
  - `track_L/R` needs custom properties `track_length_m` and `link_pitch_m`.
- The mount-yaw pivot must equal the hull origin (the sim's muzzle model), or the validator must check the muzzle arc as this spike did.
- Muzzle-fit rule: see the tank landmine. The check needs the user's rule decision before it can be written.
- Infantry muzzle height depends on posture: 1.14 m idle (low ready), 1.05 m kneeling. The sim's `infantry_muzzle_m` is a flat 1.4. Validate the muzzle socket against a standing-aim reference pose, not idle, or raise a sim question.

**Slice 20 (workbench):**
- The views and strips here (8 named views, 8 phases per clip) are the sheet shape to reproduce in our renderer.
- The articulated pose driver needs: turret yaw, gun pitch, HMG yaw and pitch, wheel spin by distance over radius, track UV offset by distance over link pitch, and deploy progress. They are exactly the `set_pose` and `set_deploy` above.
- Edge wear and dirt here come from Cycles-only nodes (Bevel, world-z gradient). Our renderer won't have them, so bake them into a texture or vertex colour at bundle bake.

**Slice 21 (infantry):**
- Port the spike's method, not its code:
  - body shells for clothing;
  - bone-parented rigid kit, skinned at bake;
  - the rifle-frame IK hold rig baked to FK for the library clips;
  - authored kneel and prone.
- Fix list from this spike and the critique:
  - The uniform is still skin-tight and muscular. It needs a looser shell (bigger offset on the legs, a smoothing weight) or a modelled blouse and trousers.
  - Boots are a rigid boot and sole on `foot_*` plus a shaft shell. They still read chunky and need a modelled last.
  - Prone: the left hand falls 0.18 m short of the handguard and the shoulders hunch.
  - The run still twists at 45%.
  - Gloves are 6.7k triangles.
- `infantry_kit.py` must build recon (DMR) and AT (launcher) variants on the same holds. The AT launcher needs its own shoulder-fire hold. Tint by mask.
- Manifest entries go in `third_party` with `accepted_by: "user, 2026-09-25"` for `UAL1_Standard.glb` and the UBC male `.gltf`/`.bin` (hashes above). UAL2 is **not** covered by the acceptance and is not needed.

**Slice 22 (vehicles):**
- Port the scripted-parts technique: prisms with bevels, bolted skirt panels, a convex-hull track band with UV-scroll links, and procedural woodland camo with wear and dirt, baked.
- Get the user's call on `tank_muzzle_local_m` first.
- Budget LODs on the wheels: 22 × 716 triangles is half the tank.
- The HMG and cupola stand 0.4 m above the 2.4 m hit box. Accept that as presentation, or say so in the catalog tolerance.
- Truck deploy phases as above. Wreck variants were not tried.
