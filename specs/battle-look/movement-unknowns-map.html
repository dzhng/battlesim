Movement unknowns map

Company of Heroes-style infantry and vehicle weight and push classes, added to specs/battle-look on 2026-09-26. Every decision below is a given for the slices it names. The earlier interview is unknowns-map.html.

1 · Known knowns: the settled ground

  • Soldiers are offsets from the squad centre. Soldier {offset, formation} sits in the squad's frame (crates/sim/src/units.rs:22). A soldier's world position is the squad position plus the rotated offset, at the squad's height (units.rs:237). Nothing moves a soldier except reform (movement.rs:102).
  • Pathing is one A* route per unit on a 2 m grid, per mover class and per side's knowledge (navigation.rs). Replanning happens on a goal move, a stall, or a revision change where the route no longer fits (movement.rs:117–187).
  • Cover is judged per soldier, but nobody seeks it. Blast, spread and sensing already read each soldier's position (damage.rs:47–109, weapons.rs:935). A cover source widens incoming scatter (cover_spread).
  • Hits are physical, in flight. A round hits a soldier only if its path crosses his capsule over the tick (flight/mod.rs:176–230). Garrisoned soldiers stand at slots, and the building's cover strength widens scatter (garrison.rs:553).
  • Squad fire: range and the decision to engage are per squad, from one muzzle point (weapons.rs:450). Every living soldier fires his own round; a soldier whose line doesn't solve skips (weapons.rs:918–1016).
  • No mass or class exists anywhere. The vehicles are the tank and the supply truck. MoverClass {Infantry, Vehicle} and the table PropKind::blocks are at contract/map.rs:98–134. Props can be added or removed but not moved (world/mod.rs:256).
  • New wrecks trigger reroutes: sight in the fog sweep (every 6 ticks, battle.rs:1249) or contact within 2 m bumps the side's revision, and a route that no longer fits replans the next tick.
  • Presentation is ready: each soldier's position and id are published, and the renderer firewall forbids formation-slot placement (the README's Firewalls).

3 · Unknown knowns: what was extracted

  • Workbenches are scripted, not interactive. They are non-interactive scenario runners, like ~/dev/game's weave harness (crates/sim/src/bin/weave_shots.rs). Each named scenario runs deterministically and outputs PNG flip-books and GIFs. Their purpose is for Claude to verify pathfinding and cover with clean visuals, red dots and grey boxes; the user reviews last. This reshaped the first slice into a scenario runner, not a tool.
  • The throwaway mock found three rules the sim must follow (throwaway/infantry-mock/):
    • a cover spot sits behind the prop's face, along its normal away from the threat; otherwise thin sandbags and fences are rejected;
    • a soldier's lane falls back to the corridor wherever his offset would cross an obstacle;
    • the corridor must be real A* with clearance.
  • Squads are the unit of command. Selection, orders and the HUD stay per squad; only the soldiers move individually.
