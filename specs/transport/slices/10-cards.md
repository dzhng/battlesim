# 10 · The Seats row and passenger cards

**Owns:** D19, D20, L7. **Depends on:** 03, 06 (needs carrier holds in the observation).

Load `game-ui` and `renderer` first.

## Contract
- **Seats row.** Every own carrier's card always shows a state row reading `SEATS n/N`, in compact and detailed view alike, even when empty.
  - There are no pips (D19).
  - The number goes in the row's words through `n`, the way `SUPPLY 412` does (`web/src/battle/present/panelRows.ts:47-51`).
  - `n` is the sum of the living members of own squads whose `hold.host` is this carrier. `N` is `carriage.seats` from the catalog.
- **Compact view.** Compact cards hide every section today (`web/src/battle/present/hud.css:1281-1284`). The Seats row is the one exception.
- **Passenger cards.** Each passenger squad's compact card stacks under its carrier's card, whatever view the carrier's card is in. Passenger cards are always compact, even with Space held or hovered.
  - Every callout today is anchored at its own unit (`readouts.tsx:191-202`), and layout hides a card that can't fit (`readoutLayout.ts:39-41`).
  - Passengers share the carrier's anchor and break `nearerAnotherAnchor` (`:254-265`).
  - So nested callouts are a new layout rule in `readouts.tsx`, `readoutLayout.ts` and `readoutDetails.ts`: they extend the existing callout, not a parallel one.
- **The enemy card never shows seats** (`enemyPanel`, `panelRows.ts:509`).
- **New state icon** for Seats, generated by `packages/scene-assets/src/icons.ts` (`asset check` fails if it's missing).

## What a human can see

  - compact and detailed views;
