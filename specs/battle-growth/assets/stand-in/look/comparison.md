# Stand-in look review evidence

The selected candidate is an opaque, side-coloured plain box with a fully
rough material and no cast sun shadow. The colour is the existing HUD enemy
or observer-side role, not a new loading colour. A neutral dead wreck remains
terrain cover. The ledger owns the choices and rejected alternatives.

`comparison.png` places the default, the three candidates and the loaded
model beside each other at matched cameras. The native selected frames cover
vehicle/squad, open/fog and near/far; `held` and `model` use the same paused
observation. The squad's open control suppresses fog through the existing lab
control. The vehicle open view is a real open field. Far cells in the sheet
are enlarged detail crops; the native files preserve actual game scale.

The fresh image-only critique is retained verbatim. It recognises the stronger
side proxy and placement, but still sees crate/post ambiguity and cannot prove
that the still image communicates loading. This is a review candidate, not a
blessed scene baseline or a claim of resolved player interpretation.

The strict pixel comparison uses the first-contact vehicle's near fog view.
Its baseline pair is retained in `baseline/`. The region in
`production-pixel-check.json` comes from the physical pick, the existing CPU
camera projection and the fixture's sun, including the former shadow and a
small raster fringe. All held-frame changes lie there. After the hard swap,
the scene pixels match exactly; only the FPS digits differ. The aggregate
comparisons against the default and loaded model are retained as JSON.
