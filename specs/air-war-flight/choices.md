# Air-war flight: choices

Decisions made where the spec was silent, appended each pass (`audit-choices`). Decisions the user made live in [the map](map.md).

| Pass | Choice | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| write-spec | A ground-missile pass flies the strafe profile (nose-on, under `strafe_ceiling_m`). | Settled | The user described the missile pass as "the same type of strafe maneuver where its nose is pointed at the enemy". D13 left its ceiling unset. |
| write-spec | A shadow is thrown from the aircraft's actual height, along the sun. | Settled | A fixed offset would leave a diving jet's shadow floating. D2's one height source still covers the ghost and cruise height. |
| write-spec | Jets get an attitude slice (14) that pitches them along their path. | Settled | Today the attitude comes from acceleration, so dives draw level. |
| write-spec | A new jet test map, `air-jet` (about 3×3 km). | Settled | All four drafts found the 640×520 m air lab map is crossed in about 3.5 s at jet speed. |
| 00 | During the off-map delay the own unit is published with `traversing` and `ingress_remaining` (`units.rs:312-334`, `observation.rs:494-560`). The presentation draws only unit records, so an approach needs its own record. | Settled: the spec's `AirTransit` | Slice 12 replaces `traversing` with an own-side `AirTransit` record; slice 13 draws it. No phantom unit. |
| 02 | Jet afterburner state ramps linearly over the authored ramp time; the 100 m floor is an immediate safety clamp if a jet starts below it. | Settled | The floor is a hard invariant while alive, and a linear ramp reaches each endpoint exactly at the authored duration. |
| 05 | A motor's `top_speed_mps` is a world-frame cap. A round launched faster than its top speed coasts at its launch speed; slice 07 authors the ground missile's top speed above jet speed for its “+200 m/s” look. | Settled | The motor never actively brakes a carried launch. |
| 05 | Launch-solve cache cost: 1,000 stationary exact-cache hits retired **1,959,600 instructions**; 1,000 solves with unique carrier velocities retired **32,745,305 instructions**. | accepted: only aircraft shots miss, and they are rare | Moving shooters make exact-float keys unique; the cache remains because the repeated ground path is cheap. |
| 00 | `interception` names only `Missile` (`contract/src/weapons.rs`). | Settled | Slice 04: an interceptor engages a round through the target row its `interception` names, never a row picked by height. |
| 00 | No saved encounter relies on the four-sides spawn: encounters place units directly (`contract/src/encounter.rs`). | Settled | Slice 12 deletes the spawn without any encounter work. |
| 00 | All 19 fixed-wing family scripts call `vehicle_export.run_disabled` (`blender/vehicle_export.py:242`). Blender wasn't run in the sandbox; it is installed at `/Applications/Blender.app`. | Settled | Slice 16 builds them. |
| 00 | The test map `air-jet` is 3×3 km with `render_margin_m` 2000. The lab camera frames any fixture's field from its map's `size_m`, and scenes read the field size the same way. | Settled | One owner for each field's size: the map metadata. |
| 01 | Helicopter air routing margin is the largest admitted `LowAir` hull horizontal half extent, so the tower flight test measures clearance against that margin. | Settled | Splitting rotor and jet rules removes the old all-air `hull_limits.air` routing bound; the helicopter grid now uses the actual low-air catalog envelope. |
| 01 | The `/air` flight encounter holds the gun jeep at tick 830 (was 860). | Settled | With the helicopter routing margin at 10 m (was 11.7 m) the helicopter reaches its goal sooner, and one jeep burst landed twice before the old hold. Holding at 830 stops the burst after two shots, so exactly one round strikes: the lab's "held after one hit" claim holds in the browser and in `air_flight_encounter.rs`. |
| 01 | The helicopter routing margin is the `hull_limits.low_air.half_length_m` data value, not a catalog scan. | Settled | A catalog-dependent rule made the sim test catalog and the full-catalog browser lab route differently; one low-air data limit keeps both paths identical. |
| 04 | Re-recorded the pinned ground digests affected by 3D reach and target ranking. | Settled: no existing pinned rows changed | The full simulation, exact-number and parity checks stayed byte-identical; the new 3D body distance only changes cases with meaningful height separation, while ground fixtures remain 2D. |
| 04 | An interceptor engages a round with whichever of its air rows reaches farther. | Provisional | A dedicated interception row would be cleaner if interception rows ever diverge; slice 04 has only the `Missile` class and needs no new weapon row. |
| 03 | An idle jet stores the last explicit move goal in `AirState::last_goal` and orbits that fixed point after the order is gone; if it has never had a goal, it orbits its current position. | Settled | Slice 03 requires idle circling at the point where the jet lost its goal while keeping one owner for the remembered point. |
| merge | `test_jet` is its own test-catalog row, with the F-16 art and that art's hull and fixed cannon geometry. | Provisional: slice 16 gives it a test model | Every other test unit has its own `test_*` model, but there is no test jet model yet. Borrowing the helicopter's art failed the model-fit check, since its door gun turns. |
| 12 | `AirTransit` is a side-scoped observation group with one row per jet edge event; `dir` is an `in`/`out` tag and `edge_tick` is the authoritative spawn or crossing tick. | Settled | The sim must expose approach and departure without creating an off-map unit or leaking transit to the opposing side; a compact publication group keeps that boundary explicit. |
| 15 | Su-34 gains its iron-bomb and air-to-air mounts now but retains its SEAD mount until pass 3 enables Su-30SM2. | Settled | Removing it in this pass would leave Eastern with no enabled SEAD jet. |
| 08-09 | A bomb pass holds its selected weapon through the release tick; powered ground missiles may launch whenever their normal seeker/trajectory assessment succeeds during the pass. | Settled | This prevents the finite bomb store changing the pass to a gun on the same tick as release, while keeping a missile's on-board guidance and nose-on gun rules independent. |
| 11 | Attack-move target pickup chooses the retained enemy track nearest the current leg, and a pass keeps its target and aim point until pull-out completes. | Settled | The order layer has side knowledge but no world query; a leg projection gives deterministic pickup while preserving the D18 promise that a pass is never abandoned halfway. |
| 08-09 | The catalog's fixed-wing smoke battle uses a 3,000×2,000 m open map and places its ground aim on the pass line. | Settled | The existing 300 m edge guard would otherwise turn a jet away on the 600 m-deep catalog map before its fixed gun can bear; helicopter and ground smoke fixtures keep their original map. |
| 10 | Dogfight height is sampled once per jet from the authored band using the mixed battle seed and unit id; pursuit uses a 1.5 s lag and a head-on merge turns two seconds behind each jet before rejoining pursuit. | Settled | The band is data-owned and the per-jet stream keeps replays deterministic; the short derived merge turn makes a head-on pass read as a break-away rather than a collision. |
| 18 | The dogfight mark is a fighter on an enemy's tail: a large plan-view plane and a smaller one ahead of its nose. | Provisional: the weakest state mark at 12 px | Of six candidates shown blind at native size, it alone read as aircraft ("a jet chasing something"). Two equal planes read as a smudge or sort arrows, and nose-to-nose chevrons as a bow tie. Unprimed critiques still call it mush in amber at native size. A bigger state slot would help every mark; that would be a panel-wide change. |
| 18 | A panel name's mark (an unidentified report's dish or `?`) is drawn in the rows' own icon slot, so it and its word stand in the rows' columns. The slot overhangs the name line, so the line's height is unchanged. | Settled | The misalignment came from the name drawing its mark as a bare 10 px icon with its own margins; one slot for every row icon removes the cause. The panels scene pins it. |
| user | **Jet ground shadow (user chose B, 2026-10-14):** jets at altitude cast no sun shadow. Instead, a soft silhouette shadow lies on the ground directly beneath each jet, turned with its heading, fainter and softer the higher it flies. Helicopters keep their real sun-cast shadow. | Settled | From 250 m, the true shadow lands about 300 m away along the sun (40 deg elevation), so on screen it read as disconnected from its jet. The beneath-shadow ties a jet to its ground position and shows its height (the slice 01 readability finding). |
