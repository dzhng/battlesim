# Air-war flight: choices

Decisions made where the spec was silent, appended each pass (`audit-choices`). Decisions the user made live in [the map](map.md).

| write-spec | A shadow is thrown from the aircraft's actual height, along the sun. | Settled | A fixed offset would leave a diving jet's shadow floating. D2's one height source still covers the ghost and cruise height. |

| user | **Jet ground shadow (user chose B, 2026-10-14):** jets at altitude cast no sun shadow. | shadow | The beneath-shadow follows the altitude layer (`UnitType::high_air`, from the type's mobility layer), never the aircraft kind. It is an effect instance (`shadow`) drawn first in the effect pass: a quad in the ground's plane, shading only surfaces within `ground_band_m` of that plane, so units standing in it stay unshaded. Its shape is a procedural fighter arrowhead (near-delta wing spanning the plan, short tapered nose, tailplanes merged into the wing's trailing corners) stretched to the model's plan box, not the model's real outline. High-air models leave the sun's casters entirely, so they also lose their self-shadowing (wings on fuselage). | Provisional | One record and one shader branch serve both clients through the exported effect pipeline, with no new pass. A real outline would need a per-model top-down bake in both clients; once blurred at height, the procedural plan reads as a jet. The self-shadow loss shows only in close-ups. Keeping it would need a receiver-side cap on how far a shadow reaches, which would change every shadow. |

| passes | **A pass's line-up is flown from where the jet is, and its dive begins only nose-on.** `LineUp` flies straight on until a full-rate turn in on the aim, on the aim's side, would leave a run-in of at least the dive distance (2 s of flight: the aim lies that far along a tangent from the turn's circle). The new `TurnIn` phase then holds that turn, re-deriving the approach bearing from the jet to the aim every tick, until the nose is on the aim (`weapons::nose_on_bearing`, the fixed gun's own cone) within the run-in, and the dive begins; the bearing at that moment is the line the dive, release and pull-out fly. A `TurnIn` pushed inside its own turn circle (by the edge guard) goes back to `LineUp`. `pass::advance` takes the jet's `FixedWingLimits` for its turn radius. No new number: the run-in, cone, ceilings, floor and release solver are the ones already there. | Settled | Closeout trace: a pass's bearing was fixed when it began, at the end of the previous pull-out; the U-turn carried the jet about 290 m off that line and `LineUp` dived on distance alone (≤ 2 s of flight) with the nose 40–50° off, so the 2° gun never bore again and the release check passed from the wrong side. Turning in only with room rules out the other freeze, since turning toward an aim inside the turn circle circles it forever; committing to the turn once begun stops a marginal turn-in from dithering between the two phases. `repeated_passes_each_dive_in_nose_on_and_fire` pins it. |
