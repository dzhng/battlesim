The checkpoint had to keep the original village physical opening and learned side-specific ground through the structural changes. The historical screenshots are a parity reference, not approved art. Framings, viewport and seed were unchanged. Shipping publications and ground cells are checked separately by the [village script regression](../building-aggregate/README.md#cutover-of-existing-scenarios).

All 17 ground captures were compared with the historical production captures. The largest pixelmatch ratio is 0.00051 (red-side inspector); village default, field and full blue inspector have zero mismatch. These numbers locate changes; they do not grade art.

## Fresh critique

An unprimed agent inspected all 17 full captures, their centre crops and six extra track crops, and compared five against history. It found no visible regression specific to the candidate. These defects are all present historically and remain open:

- The full blue inspector is almost entirely a blue-grey striped surface; it is not usable as composed-scene evidence.
- Bright vegetation shows through crater interiors and rims.
- The dense crater field has abrupt rectangular edges and repeated aligned bowls.
- Large blank red/white bars sit away from infantry, and white bars appear among trees and fields.
- A repeating blue-grey strip behind the trees reads as a patterned wall.
- Shallow road craters read as flat dark smears.
- Tracks are faint, with vegetation visible through them, though both bands are continuous.
- One narrow crater contour looks like a layered cutout (not classified against history).

Dark angular regions in the inspectors read correctly as building shadows, and infantry show complete bodies and rings with no new clipping.

This accepts only the restored physical and learned-ground checkpoint and its bounded parity. It does not accept the art or the defects above, which belong to the visual pass and evidence rig.
