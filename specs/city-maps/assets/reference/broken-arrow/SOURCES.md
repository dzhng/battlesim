# Broken Arrow composition references

Two screenshots of a Mixed-style map in Broken Arrow, shared by the user on 2026-09-30 as inspiration for map composition. **For judging our layouts only: never ship, bake or trace them.** The images belong to Slitherine / Steel Balalaika. The files themselves are not in the repo yet; drop them here (`town-districts.jpg`, `town-overview.jpg`) if they should be kept.

## What they show

**`town-districts` (low oblique over a town):**
- Districts are single-use areas side by side: a garden suburb of detached houses on looser streets; apartment slabs in parallel rows with lawns and parking; one tall tower; an industrial compound and a container yard with large paved aprons.
- A straight main road and a rail line run through the middle as the town's spine; the yard and industry sit along it.
- Tree lines and small woods separate districts, and fields and forest begin at the last row of houses.
- Capture zones sit on districts (the industrial compound, the apartment block), not on empty ground.

**`town-overview` (higher, wider):**
- The town is a loose aggregate strung along the road and rail corridor, with fields and dense forest wedges between its districts.
- A warehouse compound stands apart at the edge, on its own access road.
- Dense, irregular forest patches interleave with open farmland all the way to the horizon; smaller clusters of houses sit around the main town.

## What we take from them

Recorded as the taste notes under "Mixed reads as a mosaic of districts" in [the map-design brief](../../../procedural-maps.md#3-unknown-knowns-taste-and-tacit-context-extracted). Rail is out of scope; the spine is a main road.
