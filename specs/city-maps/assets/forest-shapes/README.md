## Independently earned proof

The compressed native before record is `native-before.jsonl.gz`. Its raw hash
and independently generated matching after hash are in `native-records.json`;
source, probe and case identities are in `corpus-identity.json`.

All 57 records match byte for byte: five fixtures, normal/late Endurance for two
seeds, and the existing literal forest producer extents under the three retained
density rows. They cover native f64 original IDs, body poses and canopy values,
non-open native foliage values, original public f32 exports, and Endurance's seeded
units/scripts/events. This is owned rectangular source parity, not universal
recovery of historical fractional rectangle widths or a full battle/scale proof.

Seven shared contract tests, twelve native forest tests and twenty-five native
world-geometry tests pass. Genuine parser failures are preserved for decimal-token
rounding, crossing-zero rectangle detection, discarded legacy forest geometry and
intrinsically unrepresentable strokes. The source-count guards do not establish
aggregate preparation or memory admission.

After one Wasm build, the public-export-to-placement overlap tracer failed with
130 placements for 84 generated trunks. Immutable source ranges and lossless
original IDs then produced exactly the expected 84 ordered positions. All twenty
focused scenery/terrain web tests pass. This is ownership correctness, not pixel
or artwork acceptance.
