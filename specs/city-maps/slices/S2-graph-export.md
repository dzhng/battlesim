# S2: reusable template export and category coverage

**Depends on:** none. **Kind:** throwaway spike.

## Question
Can pinned Blender sources yield legal, reusable template descriptors and shared module placements with enough variety across the six categories?

## Contract it unlocks
`spikes/S2.md`: frozen source/dependency identities, legal-input and class × regional-family matrix, export/fit/byte tables and missing-source verdicts. Sources/artifacts stay in a cheap scratch worktree; nothing merges.

## API seam
Disposable `evaluate(TemplateRecipe) → ModuleInventory + PlacementRows + GeometryDescriptor` over vendored graphs and existing authored farmstead geometry. New home/tower/industrial sources are missing production inputs: labelled placeholder descriptors may test the seam, but do not prove their art. G0 turns those gaps into C16–C19 contracts.

Read instances before realization; use Blender 5.2.1's input API. Prototype legal widths/depths/floors/floor heights and exposed-edge variants. Geometry and placements share metres/local frame. Descriptor dimensions constrain parcels; arbitrary footprints are not a requirement.

## What the human can run or see
Class/family coverage and source-gap tables, templates at 30/80/250 m, physical fit and facade bay/floor overlays, export timings and shared-module counts.

## Verification
- Sweep legal signatures, seeds and LODs across all source graphs; include low attached/storefront and urban apartment ranges rather than treating every apartment graph as a tower/home.
- Measure evaluation time, unique module hashes/triangles, placement/descriptor/library bytes, warm reuse and two-run canonical identity.
- Confirm pre-realize access, street outputs off, floor-height and facade-bay/phase compatibility with Q3, edge variants and MIT attribution.
- Reproduce China's scripts from pinned inputs as an informational source parity check; unknown-source photo atlases never leave scratch.
- **Failure → scoped fallback:** patch inputs, snap template recipes to legal bay dimensions, bake fewer unique signatures or author a missing family. A deduped bounded module source may be an arm; flattening each placed building is not a fallback. Geometry/art mismatch or unaffordable library reslices at G0.
- Visual variable: legal template silhouette/fit only. Compare against matched source renders with compare-screenshots, run unprimed screenshot-critique last, and preview-shots non-blocking.

## Delegated to the implementer
Exporter access points, legal-input sweeps and throwaway dedupe implementation. Release dimensions/variety/coverage and codec are G0 outputs.

## Must stay green
Production behavior; shared instancing and provenance; nothing merges.

## Feedback that would change this slice
Insufficient category or regional fit changes the source/recipe matrix, not the meaning of a category.
