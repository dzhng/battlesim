# SA3 learning work: exact changed tiles and visibility words

**Depends on:** SA3 sparse pages/run transport and its frozen ground oracle.
**Kind:** focused failed-owner correction.

## Question and seam

Can recurring learned-ground updates scale with changed truth tiles and newly visible
ground, while preserving exact byte values, learning revisions, hidden edits, original
tile/local digest order and delivery? `KnownGround.learn(truth, visibility)` stays the
consumer seam. The [executed baseline](../assets/ground-learning-work/README.md) proves
isolated memory fit but recurring CPU work fails at 18 km.

## First proof

Use the safe 1 km full-marked field as the red/green work tracer, then the frozen
semantic corpus and native/wasm observations. Track actual instructions, container
capacity and both-side/retained-buffer overlap. A no-op must avoid walking every seen
fog cell; a single visible edit must avoid rescanning the full retained ground.

Implemented direction: truth owns a bounded latest-edit tile index; learned knowledge
compares visibility words and visits changed/newly visible tiles. Complete visible
pages may share immutable truth after exact changed-cell/stamp determination. No
unseen edit can change a learned page, and unchanged cells never acquire a new stamp.
Uniform values must remain uniform throughout copying, not expand then recompress.

The index and previous-visibility hint are transport-independent caches, outside the
battle digest. They must support unsealed direct API stimuli, grid changes, partial
edge holes, overlapping visibility changes, saturation, late reveal and source sharing
followed by copy-on-write. Their allocation participates in the full resource peak.

## Admission and acceptance

After focused parity/work proof, execute 12/15/18 km no-op, one-edit and reveal arms
serially under the 4 GiB ceiling. Target recurring learning within 16 ms; no pass over
33 ms. Initial full learning and reconnect publication have separate startup/resource
rows; their finite bound does not waive ordinary tick latency. Arbitrary-entropy and
whole Battle/wasm/browser/GPU admission remain separate SA3/S1/C07 obligations.

A failed arm stays failed and is resliced before broader allocation. No budget is
calibrated to the old work, no learned values are evicted, and no new physical rule or
artwork is introduced.

12/15/18 km uniform-field recurring stages; the [evidence owner](../assets/ground-learning-work/README.md)
records startup/serialization separately. Full arbitrary-entropy, repeated broad
reveal/churn and integrated active-battle gates stay open under SA3/S1/C07. Do not
substitute this checkpoint for their acceptance.
