# GG: ground verdicts

**Depends on:** SG1–SG6. **Kind:** gate.

## Question
Do the ground lane's assumptions survive, and what does it build to?

## Contract it unlocks
A gate, not code. It writes `GG-*` rows into `../decisions.md` and updates the affected slice files.

## API seam
The six `spikes/SG*.md` files.

## What the human can run or see
A one-page summary.

## Verification
Decide:
- the tree triangle budget per tier (also sent to G0);
- the dressing radius;
- the river minimum width and bank slope, or a reslice;
- whether shoulder and ruts are in or hue-only;
- the palette L* floor;
- the distance structure;
- the catalogue timing.

If a kill fired, reslice before C63+ starts.

Non-blocking: open the summary with [preview-shots](../../../.agents/skills/preview-shots/SKILL.md), allow about 5 minutes, then decide on the evidence and record the rationale.

**Ground look decisions gate on GG; full-extent storage/residency also inherits S0/G0.** Local source experiments can run independently, but production ground cannot assume village-sized allocations or unbounded distance textures on the selected maps. Reconcile SG5/SG1's field/tree pools and startup costs with G0 before committing their owners.

## Delegated to the implementer
None. Anything else you have to decide is a spec gap: record it in `../choices.md` under this slice.

## Must stay green
n/a

## Feedback that would change this slice
A failed ground spike reslices its owner; a changed user constraint reopens the corresponding accepted ground decision.
