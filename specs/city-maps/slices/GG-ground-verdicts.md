## Contract it unlocks
A gate, not code. It writes `GG-*` rows into `../decisions.md` and updates the affected slice files.

## API seam
The six `spikes/SG*.md` files.

## What the human can run or see
A one-page summary.

## Verification
Decide:
- the tree triangle budget per tier (also sent to G0);
- the dressing radius;
- the river minimum width and bank slope, or a reslice;
- whether shoulder and ruts are in or hue-only;
- the palette L* floor;
- the distance structure;
- the catalogue timing.

If a kill fired, reslice before C63+ starts.

Non-blocking: open the summary with [preview-shots](../../../.agents/skills/preview-shots/SKILL.md), allow about 5 minutes, then decide on the evidence and record the rationale.

**The forest and field lanes gate on GG, not G0**, because they touch no city spike's subject.
