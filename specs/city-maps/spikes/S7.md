# S7 verdict: plan-only composition trials

**Verdict:** the three types can be told apart from numbers alone, top/bottom fairness is cheap to get without mirroring, and Open and Mixed have plenty of 1,800 m approaches. Three things do not fit as briefed and need a G0 decision: Metro's building count, Metro's approaches on Small, and the Large transit target at 27 m/s.

Throwaway spike, no production code. The generator, 27 SVG overlays, PNG renders, `metrics.json` and the gallery are local and gitignored: `throwaway/s7/index.html` (script: `throwaway/s7/layout.py`, stdlib Python, about 50 s for everything).

## Question

Which numeric presets express the accepted type and size character, fairness and useful open approaches ([brief](../slices/S7-layout-composition.md), O01–O03 in the [map design](../procedural-maps.md))?

## What was tried

A seeded layout generator at exact metre scale, measured on a 20 m raster. For each type × size it ran 30 seeds with a balancing step and the same 30 without (540 layouts); seeds 1–3 are drawn.

- **Settlements** are irregular outlines. Towns and the city are cut into plots by a jittered arterial grid (300–450 m), and each plot gets a district kind with a category mix. Dimensions are identical at every size.
- **Roads:** four main roads meet at a crossroads at the playable centre. Every settlement then joins the nearest road. A per-seed "richness" adds cross links between neighbours and extra roads off the map. Crossings become junctions, river crossings become bridges, and low-priority bridges within 500 m of another are dropped.
- **Forests** follow the one forest rule; only area varies by seed. Rivers appear on 40–50% of seeds.
- **Named random streams** (sites, parcels, roads, nature, scenery, repair) as C52 asks.

Building counts are estimates: district area × category share × buildings per gross hectare (farmstead 1.5, detached 11, attached 26, apartment 5, highrise 2.5, warehouse 1.8). An attached home counts as one building per unit; counting a terrace row of five as one building would cut the attached count to a fifth.

## Fairness (O02)

**Proposed metric:** split the playable area at its horizontal midline. For town area and for forest area separately, the two halves pass when `|top − bottom| ≤ max(rel × (top + bottom), abs × playable area)`, with rel 0.10 and abs 0.4% for towns, rel 0.20 and abs 0.5% for forest.

| | Balanced | Unbalanced |
|---|---|---|
| Both pass, all 270 layouts | 98.2% | 30.0% |
| Worst cell | Mixed Small 90% | Open Small, Open Medium, Mixed Large 16.7% |
| Looser tolerance (towns 0.20, forest 0.25) | 100% | 50.4% |
| Tighter tolerance (towns 0.10, forest 0.10) | 79.6% | 19.3% |

The balancing step places each settlement and each wood in the half that currently holds less, then adds up to five small woods to the lighter half. Nothing is mirrored. The five balanced failures are all forest on Small maps, where one large wood outweighs the repair budget. Forest **count** (halves within one wood or 20%) is a poor stand-in: only 43–67% of the area-balanced layouts pass it, so area should be the metric.

## Open approaches (O03)

**Proposed criterion:** an approach is a run of open ground starting at a settlement's edge, with no town or forest for at least T metres outward, across a front at least 400 m wide. Counted for villages and larger. These are geometric candidates only; S1 proves they can be crossed and fired across.

| Type | Proposed T | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Open, Mixed | 1,800 m | Approaches in both halves in 93% (Open Small), 97% (Mixed Small) and 100% (the rest) of layouts. Median 7–43 per map, median front 660–840 m. The centre has one in every layout. |
| Metro Medium, Large | 1,800 m on the city | The city has one in 80% / 100% of layouts, in both halves in 43% / 100%. |
| Metro Small | 1,200 m on the city | 1,800 m exists in 10% of layouts; 1,200 m in 93%, both halves in 87%. |

A 6 km map with a city over a third of its area cannot hold 1,800 m of open ground beside it. At 77% urban (a separate 10-seed probe with the city running off the edges), 1,200 m approaches to the city survive in 10% of Small and Medium layouts and 50% of Large, and the usable plain falls to 6–10%. Metro "up to 80%" and "usable plains at weapon range" do not both hold.

## Surroundings margin

**Proposed: 1,000 m on every side at every size**, holding woods, a few hamlets and villages, and the roads running on to the outer edge, all visual-only. Rendered area is then 1.78× / 1.56× / 1.44× the playable area. A margin of a full weapon range would be 2.56× on Small. In these trials the ring adds up to 2,615 buildings and 81,639 trunks on Large Metro, and up to 168,775 trunks on Large Open. The 1,000 m figure comes from that cost arithmetic; no camera framing was measured.

## Transit

Road distance from where each main road meets the edge to the playable centre, timed at road speed plus 15 s for planning and turns. Worst edge per layout:

| Size | At 27 m/s | At the 30.56 m/s cap | Within 180 s |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 127–129 s | 114–116 s | all |
| Medium | 164–167 s | 146–150 s | all |
| Large | 201–205 s | 179–183 s | none at 27 m/s; 43–60% at the cap |

Road detour over the straight line is 0.3–2.5%. The Large result is arithmetic, not layout: 5 km at 27 m/s is 185 s before any allowance. The first trial aimed the main roads at an off-centre town and gave 203–237 s on Large (detour up to 20%), which is why the roads now meet at the centre. Starting from the exact edge midpoint instead of the road adds an off-road leg at a third of road speed: 208–267 s on Large.

Every settlement was road-connected in all 270 layouts. 85–100% of land is within 1,000 m of a road, 52–95% within 500 m (Open is the low end).

## Risks

- **Metro building count.** 44,617–67,572 buildings on Large (83,099–92,452 at 77% urban) against stress samples of 500, 1,500 and 3,000. Detached homes at 11 per hectare drive it. Holding the city at its Small size instead would give roughly 16% urban on Large and about a third of the buildings, but Large Metro would then look like Mixed.
- **Trunks.** Up to 266,241 in the playable area on Large Open, 102,765 on Large Metro, before the margin.
- **Streets.** C53 has to cut 536–796 km of local streets on Large Metro (about 4,900–7,200 block-edge segments) on top of 691–983 plan pieces; curve densification multiplies both.
- **The transit target fixes the road skeleton.** Every map has a four-arm crossroads at its centre. Seeds vary exits by only 2–6% of the edge, plus bends and the secondary network. That is less variety than M09 implies.
- **Bridges.** A river through the Metro city yields 6–13 bridges even after thinning; C52 needs a bridge budget and must re-check connectivity after dropping any (this spike had to re-add some).
- **Placement can fail.** Metro leaves up to four satellites unplaced; C52 needs the bounded-retry diagnostics it already plans.

## Still open for G0 and S1

- Does the Metro city grow with the map, and what building count can the sim and renderer hold? That decides Metro's urban share.
- Is one attached unit one building, or one terrace row?
- The transit target against road speed: accept about 200 s on Large at 27 m/s, raise the speed, or measure from the road exit at the cap.
- Whether margin scenery has any physical role, and the camera framing that sets its depth.
- S1 must prove the approach candidates physically: infantry sees about 600 m, so the 1,800 m criterion mostly serves vehicles and long-range weapons.

## What still looks wrong

From my own review and one unprimed reviewer of nine overlays:

- **Open Large reads as a thin Mixed.** Its extra small towns carry street grids and a centre district. Open should probably cap at villages outside the centre.
- **Mixed Large has no dominant town.** The centre holds only 22–36% of urban area there; six similar towns surround it.
- **A passing forest split can look uneven.** 3.9 against 3.1 km² passes the 0.20 tolerance and reads as a quarter more. Tightening to 0.10 needs a stronger repair step (79.6% pass today).
- **Area balance is not character balance.** One layout has the river and every bridge in the top half and every satellite in the bottom. Balanced placement also tends to put towns in opposite corners, which looks rotationally symmetric.
- **Road hierarchy.** Some towns reach the main roads only along a dirt track, because each settlement joins whatever road is nearest.
- The city outline is a rounded rectangle on a uniform grid, the fixed-size core looks small on Large, forests ignore roads, and extra edge roads end in short stubs.

## User decisions after this spike (2026-09-30)

Recorded as M18–M21 in [the map-design brief](../procedural-maps.md#closed-decisions). The 1,200 m exception proposed above for Metro Small is **rejected**: one 1,800 m criterion holds for every type and size, and Metro's urban share comes down until Small meets it. The remaining proposals (40–55% as Metro's starting ceiling, the fairness metric, the 1 km surroundings margin, a dominant town for Mixed, more varied roads) are accepted as the starting presets for C52, to be confirmed by its own gallery.
