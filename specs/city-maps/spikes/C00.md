**Verdict:** the pure physical catalogue/materialization seam passes its focused native/wasm proof. This unlocks C01's aggregate consumer review. It does not accept real-source floors, entrances, facade fit, template coverage, joins in art or complete G0.

## One physical owner

`contract::templates` owns local oriented-box parts, named facade spans, explicit bay pitch/phase, entrances and declared internal join pairs. Translation and rotation materialize physical records once; scaling is absent. Height derives from the highest part top and floor count from the authored datum list. Materials, module placements, LODs and source readiness belong outside this descriptor and its identity.

`TemplateGeometryCatalog` validates inputs, sorts named rows, normalizes signed zero and computes SHA256 over the canonical physical template array. Its hash is verified on reload. The compiled map will pin this identity once in C01; no appearance hash is introduced into battle data. A frozen identity file pinned the source, inputs and reports. Raw evidence: tag `city-maps-evidence-2026-09-30`.

The original village supplies three exact box shapes. It supplies no physical floor, entrance or Q3 bay data. Missing facts remain absent and `require_complete()` rejects them before a consumer requiring those facts can select them. Existing capacity-driven slots remain owned by the current simulation until C40; no art trim or window script becomes physical authority.

The allocation allowance is **65,536 cumulative XY bay positions per descriptor**, at most 1 MiB of coordinate storage. A whole 18 km square at nominal 3 m pitch has about 24,000 positions and fits this technical bound. It does not select building dimensions or ratify source pitch/fit. Whole catalogue residency and whole-map compilation remain their owning scale gates.

The initial unequal-height compound was a failed arm: a full-height internal boolean hid the taller part's upper exterior wall. Its original input, output, source snapshots and identity remain [frozen separately](../assets/template-geometry/rejected-unequal-join/README.md). The current supported join contract rejects that case. The legal equal-height API fixture is explicitly different data, not a repinned pass for the failed arm. Independent parts retain arbitrary positive extents, elevations and yaw.

Numeric admission is translation invariant: joins and coverage compare relative centres/offsets, and vertical allowance depends on height rather than absolute XY or Z origin. Bay indices remain exact consecutive f64 integers; source and transformed points are checked for distinct strictly interior positions before any bay vector allocation. The [rejected numeric receipt](../assets/template-geometry/rejected-numeric/README.md) preserves the original accepted-invalid outputs. The correction has separate current proof identity (`numeric-correction.json`); the original valid complete records remain unchanged. Independent source review found no remaining actionable issue in this correction.

The overlay diagram (`asymmetric-overlay.svg`) drew only tested emitted spans, bay points and entrance normals. It is a diagnostic diagram, not S2/S5 source-fit or appearance evidence.
