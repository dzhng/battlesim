# S1: what a full-size map costs the simulation (first pass)

**Status:** first measurements on 2026-09-30, before the generator exists. The maps are hand-built probes (`throwaway/city/plan.py`: a jittered lattice of country roads and dirt tracks every 1.5 km and forest blobs over 15% of the ground, compiled by `mapgen lower`), with no buildings. The tool is `cargo run -p sim --release --example city_report <map.json> [sim-seconds] [reach] [units-per-side]`. Rerun it on generated maps as soon as C52 produces them, and with buildings after C53.

## The question
Which costs fail at the playable extents, with real forests and roads on the map?

## Small (6 × 6 km), 63,332 trees, six units a side

| Stage | Before | After this pass |
|---|---|---|
| World build | 13.9 s, 23 G instructions | 0.09–0.2 s, 1.1 G |
| Battle build | — | 0.1–0.3 s, 1.2 G, 36 MB resident |
| Steady tick (units moving) | — | p50 0.1–0.3 ms, p95 4–13 ms |
| Planning twelve 900 m routes (tick 1) | 160 s | 12 s |
| Planning twelve edge-to-edge routes | did not finish in 12 minutes | not rerun |

Wall times were taken on a loaded machine; instructions are the comparable number.

## What was wrong, and fixed here
- **Tree placement scanned every prop for every candidate trunk** (quadratic in trees). It now asks the prop index. Digest-neutral.
- **The planner scanned every nonuniform rectangle on the map for every cell it expanded** (one rectangle per tree: about 2 million instructions per expansion). The rectangles are now bucketed and a cell tests only those within reach; the answer is identical. Digest-neutral.

## What still fails: route planning
After those fixes one 900 m plan still costs 0.3–3 s:
- **Three pre-checks walk every non-default cell on the map on every plan** (`separated`, `uniform_segment`, `search_bound`): 1.27 million cells here, 100–900 ms a plan. They prove shortcuts that only hold on mostly empty maps. Their map-wide parts depend on the mover, not on the route, so they can be computed once per mover and cached, and their rectangle tests can read only the rectangle.
- **The search expands 65,000–790,000 cells for a 900 m move.** The fastest-route estimate assumes road speed everywhere, and road speed is now up to 3.4 times off-road speed, so the search floods outward. This is what [SA2](../slices/SA2-counted-route-integration.md) answers: plan in counted slices over several ticks while the unit holds, and route long legs (over 2 km) over the road graph with local searches only at each end.

Everything except planning already fits a 6 km map. Planning does not fit at any length, so **SA2 is the next blocker for a playable full-size map**, ahead of parcels and buildings.
