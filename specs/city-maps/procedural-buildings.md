# Procedural buildings from ProceduralBuildingsThreeJS: unknowns map

Walked with the user on 2026-09-28. This owns retained building-art/source lessons. The [completed procedural-map walk](procedural-maps.md), dated 2026-09-30, supersedes real-geography scope, per-map Q-D baking and map-size assumptions. Its runtime choice uses finite reusable templates; one regional family is selected per invented map. The README and current slices own execution.

**Source.** [chirovisuals' tweet](https://x.com/chirovisuals/status/2104548658171834749) links [github.com/achrefelouafi/ProceduralBuildingsThreeJS](https://github.com/achrefelouafi/ProceduralBuildingsThreeJS), which is MIT, with a [live demo](https://proceduralbuildings.chirostudio.xyz/). It has three procedural buildings made with Blender geometry nodes: a Paris Haussmann block (`FrenchBuilding.blend`), an NYC prewar corner building (`NYC_CornerBuilding.blend`) and a Chinese apartment block (`CN_ApartmentBuilding.blend`). They run live in three.js. x.com returns 402 to a plain fetch, so the tweet text came from api.fxtwitter.com.

## 1. Known knowns: settled ground

**The repo**
- Its `LICENSE` is MIT (© 2026 mohamedachrefelouafi).
- Its textures are ambientCG sets plus Poly Haven HDRIs, all CC0 (its README, "Blender files").
- The `.blend` files were opened headless in our Blender 5.2.1 (they were saved in 5.1).
  - None has linked libraries or asset-library markers.
  - Each embeds the author's own generator scripts: `NYC_gn_dsl.py` and `CN_gn_dsl.py` build node graphs from Python, and the `CN_0x_kit_*.py` scripts build the kit meshes.
  - Default sign text is generic: NYC "HOTEL", China 幸福公寓.
- The interior atlases `blender/textures/misc/apartmentinterios.png` and `businesses.png` have **no stated source** and look AI-generated. They are lamp-lit night photos with invented shop names.
- **Paris** was hand-ported to TypeScript. **NYC and China** are dumped to `graph.json` and run by a general geometry-nodes evaluator (`src/nyc/gn.ts`), matched to Blender to the float bit (`npm run verify:*`). We don't need `gn.ts`: Blender runs the graphs at bake.
- **Materials** are world-space triplanar ambientCG sets with FBM grime, applied through `onBeforeCompile`. China box-projects UVs in its `CN_Finalize` graph, and `CN_10_export.py` already bakes LOD0–2 plus `UCX_` collision.
- **Interiors:** each window gets a small room mesh, and a flat-perspective lookup into a 2×5 atlas picks the room by hashing the instance position (Paris `FR_Interior_Rooms`, NYC `NYC_Interior`, and the NYC shop interiors). China's rooms and balconies are modelled geometry.

**Our side** (cited):
- The battle camera (`fixtures/village.json` `presentation.camera`) has a 0.8 rad vertical field of view and 25–2,000 m zoom. Pitch rises from 0.22 rad at 25 m to 0.85 rad at 65 m and beyond.
- The renderer is TypeGPU/WebGPU with no three.js (`research.md:16`).
- Blender is pinned to 5.2.1 (`web/asset.mjs:72`).

**Evidence** (gitignored): `throwaway/evidence/city-maps-chiro/sheet.jpg`, regenerated by [`assets/render_rts_views.py`](assets/render_rts_views.py) (run it headless on each vendored `.blend`: `Blender --background <file>.blend --python assets/render_rts_views.py -- <tag> <outdir>`) shows NYC, China and Paris at 30, 80 and 250 m from our camera, with material emission zeroed.

## 3. Unknown knowns: what the user revealed

- **Lived-in facades are the point.** The Chinese enclosed-balcony shot (laundry behind glass, AC units, grilles, rust streaks) is the reference look. That reshaped Q-E twice and forced L3.
- **Ground floors must have interiors.** Storefronts are part of the street read.
- **War-zone lighting:** interiors are unlit, not cozy. That's why the atlas is replaced.
- **"Don't do more than the repo does":** match their mechanisms, don't invent new ones. This scopes L3 and Q-E.
- **Performance gates everything:** "if it's performant" is the condition on interiors (O-1).
