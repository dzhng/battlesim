# Surroundings lane: country round the old maps

A separate session works this lane in parallel with the others. The village, the labs and the benchmark fields are small arenas with nothing beyond their edge. This lane gives each one generated country round it, and proves the arena's own battle did not change. It is placement and proof, judged by digests, not by eye.

## The contract

Every shipped map has real ground beyond its arena: terrain, roads that carry on, woods, and where it fits a settlement, generated by the map generator round a reserved arena. Inside the reservation nothing moves: the same map content, the same battle digests and replays, the same scene checks. Each change that cannot be avoided is named. ([C56](slices/C56-fixture-surroundings.md) → [C34 village](slices/C34-village-surroundings.md) · [C35 labs](slices/C35-lab-surroundings.md) · [C36 benchmarks](slices/C36-benchmark-surroundings.md))

## How to work

Read [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md): narrow checks only, no full gate, no frozen-record tests. The battle digest is the proof that an arena did not change: capture each map's digests before, compare after. A saved map stores a building as its template and frame, and its `SOURCES.json` pins its content hash ([fixtures guide](../../fixtures/README.md)). `mapgen inspect` draws a plan as a picture; look at each map's surroundings once before committing them. Branch from main, merge main often, push small green passes. Add an Outcome to each slice file and decisions to [`choices.md`](choices.md) under a heading for the slice. Scratch output goes in gitignored `throwaway/`.

**One GPU, shared.** Several sessions are working at once. Run every scene through the GPU lock in the main checkout (the README's Checks section has the command), never two at a time, and keep heavy jobs (a full Rust test run) to one at a time.
