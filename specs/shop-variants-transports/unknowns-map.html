1 · Known knowns: settled ground

  • A catalog card is one variant: id plus roster {factions, family_name, category, variant}, 152 cards in all. Buying one already sends a single card id (crates/contract/src/command.rs:119).
  • Today's shop (web/src/battle/present/purchasePicker.tsx) has one tile per family, showing the cheapest price. Hovering opens stacked info cards for every variant. Clicking the tile buys the "first affordable" variant, a hidden choice. Enter buys when exactly one family matches (purchaseFilter.ts enterChoice).
  • The card face is 64 × 88 px: role icon, silhouette, price, no text (hud.css:1189, unitFace.tsx).
  • Flattened, the largest tab holds 10 cards (US/Eastern JETS); US MBT goes from 2 tiles to 5. The grid scrolls past 45% of the viewport height.
  • The AI already uses transports. It buys squads and carriers separately, pairs them by seat count (skirmish_ai/transport.rs carrier_for, want), boards them, and unloads at a drop point outside known enemy reach.
  • Carriers: 32 card types with carriage.seats of 3, 8 or 16, including helicopters. Squads have 2–8 soldiers.
