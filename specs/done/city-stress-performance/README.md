# Cheaper simulation with identical battles

The simulation reuses derived geometry and visibility and avoids calculations whose results are not consumed. The [performance report](performance-report.md) preserves the fixed controls, measured savings, memory tradeoff and remaining benchmark limits. Its native and browser comparisons use different physical maps, so their numerical results must stay separate.

The contract is unchanged: movement admission, battle state, replay outcomes and each side’s published observation retain their values and ordering. Optimization state is derived state, never a new authority. The complete recorded battle compares both state digests and packed publications because fog output is not fully represented by the battle digest.

The [visibility cache](../../../crates/sim/src/visibility.rs) keeps complete individual observer fields, rather than fields that already contain another observer’s contribution. This distinction prevents ghost visibility when an observer moves or dies. Reuse requires unchanged eyes, sight, relevant sensor rules, body revision and cleared-ground count; clearing invalidates independently of body edits. Terrain and grid stay fixed for the cache’s lifetime. Conservative global invalidation keeps the ownership simple, at the expense of cold sweeps after unrelated geometry changes. Retained fields are bounded by the battle’s unit roster, including dead entries.

The [radius comparisons](../../../crates/sim/src/math.rs) preserve the portable norm’s decisions. Safely separated squared values avoid its cost; rounding ties and exceptional values retain the original evaluator. Actual distances used in movement and aim keep their arithmetic. The [scenery index](../../../crates/sim/src/world/props.rs) owns the union for both actual and remembered candidates, preserving bucket membership and ascending unique IDs before learning bodies.

The [movement snapshot](../../../crates/sim/src/movement/mod.rs) owns the immutable hull bounds shared by infantry for one tick. The [soldier path](../../../crates/sim/src/movement/soldier.rs) delays pure calculations until their existing consumer needs them. Neither introduces persistent invalidation state. The tests alongside these owners and the [movement/publication contracts](../../../crates/sim/tests/) protect the numerical boundaries, observer changes, physical travel and cross-target output.

Native-only hardware math was rejected because stable Wasm had no matching gain. Route-leg caching was omitted because its small measured gain did not earn extra state and arithmetic. The body-rotation trial was not promoted, and its isolated effect remains unknown because removing the route cache confounded attribution. The report retains those evidence boundaries. Gameplay tuning remains independent of these optimizations; repaired tests control optics at the test seam and preserve the current exterior-eye/interior-body garrison contract.

Several-second admission pauses and the renderer’s FPS floor remain follow-up work. Their recorded benchmark failures were retained, rather than changing requirements to accept this CPU optimization. The city-maps generation report remains preserved under its completed spec.
