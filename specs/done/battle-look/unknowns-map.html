1 · Known knowns — the settled ground

The goal

  • Make the village battle look like WARNO in summer:
    • painterly patchwork fields;
    • dense 3D grass;
    • a warm afternoon grade.
  • Take framing and scars from Defilade, effects from Broken Arrow and WARNO, and urban fog from ARMAPHRACT.
  • Build it with real, animated, modern models.

Done means friends can play a full village battle in the new look on this Mac.

Provisional numbers (tune during the build)

  • The sight shape per unit kind (front, side, rear multipliers).
  • Crater cover strength and vehicle slowdown.
  • Ricochet probability per face, speed kept after a bounce, and scatter.

  • The unseen-fog dim and cool values.
  • The camera's zoom-to-pitch curve and its zoom limits.

4 · Unknown unknowns — landmine cards

The sweep covered the simulation, the contract, web observation, the renderer, the lab viewport and input, all 20 browser scenes, the Vite and scene-runner config, and the parts of ~/dev/game being ported.

1 · Ricochets can't be decided inside flight decided
flight/mod.rs:492-610 stops at the first hit; penetration lives in damage.rs:146-158; flight has no RNG.
  • Pass a resolver into flight and re-fly the rest of the tick.
  • Re-query the collision grid, excluding the struck hull.
  • A separate Ricochet event keeps the round id (flight_load.rs:157 pins one ending per round).
  • The bounce count is digested; segments become polylines.
2 · Soldier ids leak roster size accepted
One global counter across both sides (battle.rs:239, supply.rs:188).
Publish Soldier.id as it is (F2).
3 · Craters as props cause navigation rebuild storms decided
Learning a prop rebuilds the side's navigation grid and every route check (movement.rs:43-151).
Craters live in the per-cell ground layer (D2). Cover joins the "strongest source wins" rule (damage.rs:38-77).
4 · Scar publication grows forever decided
Known props and corpses are re-sent in full every tick (battle.rs:1216, 1322).
Delta cells with resync (D2). Corpses stay as they are for now.
5 · Animation inputs are mostly new state sharp edge
No elevation, shot counter, enemy turret or segment weapon; Mount.bearing exists (weapons.rs:188).
Everything new joins Mount::digest. Values are packed as float32, so counters are exact only up to 2²⁴.
6 · Directional sight flips tests and results sharp edge
UnitSetup.yaw defaults to +X; red placed east faces away (damage.rs:107, contacts.rs:92, …); sensing.rs:27 pins exact ranges.
Fix yaws deliberately, and expect shifts in the village and endurance reports.
7 · Simulation fog vs renderer fog sharp edge
The sweep runs every 6 ticks; bearing updates after sensing (battle.rs:593-600).
  • Renderer fog uses the published bearing at the published tick, never interpolated.
  • Same foliage and prop rules.
  • The sweep shrinks its range per ray; masking afterwards saves nothing.
8 · Damage face read from the end-of-tick pose sharp edge
units.rs:371 vs Body::pose_at.
Ricochet needs the face and normal at the moment of the hit.
9 · The ported animation is timeline-driven and medieval sharp edge
~/dev/game/packages/crowd-runtime/src/instanceData.ts: per-soldier clips, horses, factions.
Write an adapter from our animation feed to clips. There are no modern assets to take.
10 · Camera uniform layouts differ, 48 vs 40 floats sharp edge
~/dev/game/packages/renderer-core/src/cameraUniform.ts vs ours.
Adopt the 48-float layout. Nothing may read stale offsets.
11 · Terrain sampling differs sharp edge
Cell-centred bilinear with 1.6× relief there; corner-sampled triangles here (terrain.rs).
Rewrite terrain, grass and scenery against our triangle export, with no exaggeration.
12 · The ported renderer has no fog sharp edge Every ported fragment shader gains the fog term, and bind group indices get renumbered.
13 · HDR and tonemapping would recolour overlays sharp edge
An rgba16float target plus AgX there (post.ts:53); direct to the canvas here.
Overlays composite after post, in display space, drawn over fog.
14 · Two resource-ownership models sharp edge
Module-level own() there; one scene registry plus trackGpuAllocations here.
Every ported allocation must be registered, or the baseline checks fail. Retune shadow constants for 0.3–1.6 km maps.
15 · Asset volume and provenance sharp edge
3.3 GB in web/public/assets/soldiers; Quaternius CC0 marked "not yet accepted"; other families have no licence notes.
Port the pipeline, not the assets. Audit provenance before copying anything.
16 · Keys pressed by scenes change meaning sharp edge
readouts.mjs, movement.mjs, village-replay.mjs, foundation.mjs; typing guard LabViewport.tsx:402.
Move the commands before WASD lands. Use the stronger typing guard, plus held-key tracking.
17 · Explicit scene framings sharp edge
Seven scenes set pitch and distance; geometry.mjs:108 is below the minimum pitch.
The zoom-to-pitch link goes in the wheel gesture only; setCamera stays raw. projectToCss must equal camera3d.
18 · Pixel checks tuned to flat shading sharp edge
sensors.mjs:44-48 (seen ground must change by less than 3), contacts.mjs, geometry.mjs, ballistics.mjs, foundation.mjs.
Retune each deliberately; never loosen one silently.
19 · Picking uses box proxies sharp edge
picking.ts:39, picking.test.ts.
The simulation's boxes stay the pick volumes, whatever the model.
20 · Asset serving sharp edge
No web/public; the production build only sees its output folder; COEP require-corp.
Use imported or public/ URLs, all served locally, including decoders.
21 · Telemetry and corpses sharp edge
GPU telemetry counts buffer bytes only; endurance has 20,000 corpses.
Count texture bytes. Corpses are static instanced poses, never skinned.

Confirm before coding
