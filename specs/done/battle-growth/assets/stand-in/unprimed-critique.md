# Unprimed critique: the stand-in's final look

A reader given only the four pictures in this folder and centre crops of
them, told that they are two before/after pairs from a strategy game's battle
view and nothing else, on 2026-11-16. Its words, trimmed to the findings
about the stand-in; findings about the HUD and the map are left out.

## Problems, most serious first

1. **The blocks cast no shadow.** The hull box and the soldiers' slabs look
   pasted onto the grass, while the carrier and riflemen in the after frames
   throw clear shadows, so a shadow appears from nothing at the swap. All
   four frames. Confidence: high.
2. **A squad's slabs merge into its carrier's box.** They share the same pale
   top and near-black side, so the group reads as one object: a table on
   legs, or a bunker with posts. Both before frames. Confidence: high.
3. **Nothing marks a block as a unit or as temporary.** No outline, no
   translucency, no tie to the HUD. The box could be a container; the slabs
   could be bollards. Both before frames. Confidence: medium.
4. **No readable heading.** The box's long axis matches the carrier, but
   front and rear look the same, and the slabs have no face. Both before
   frames. Confidence: high.
5. **The hull box overstates the nose.** It is full height where the carrier
   has a low sloping front, so the silhouette shrinks there at the swap.
   Both pairs. Confidence: medium to high.
6. **The block is the brightest and the darkest thing in the frame.** Its
   pale top and near-black sides outshout the grass and the camouflage that
   replaces them. Both pairs. Confidence: high that it is so, medium that it
   matters in motion.
7. **The slabs are bulkier than the men.** They stand in the right places
   and are noticeably wider and a little taller. Both pairs. Confidence:
   medium.
8. **A small pale peak breaks the far top edge of the hull box** in both
   before frames. Confidence: high that it is there, medium that it is a
   defect. Not explained.

9. **The squad pair does not show the squad well.** It is the vehicle pair
   with the camera shifted, so the same carrier fills both. Confidence: high.
10. **The order bar and unit card differ between before and after.** Before,
    every order button is dim and the card unfilled; after, the card is
    filled and several buttons are lit. Either the captures are not the same
    interface moment, or a unit behind a block cannot be ordered. Confidence:
    high that they differ, low on the cause.

## Before against after

- **Placement:** good. Every slab has a rifleman in the matching place, and
  the grass is identical between each before and after, so the instants
  match.
- **Size:** the box is about the carrier's envelope and at deck height, too
  generous at the nose. The slabs are too wide for a man.
- **Heading:** the long axis is right; which end is the front is not shown.
- **Deliberate or bug:** it reads as a bug, or at best as developer greybox,
  and the group can be mistaken for a single structure.

## Beside the author's own look

Items 1, 2 and 6 are plain in the pictures. Item 1 is a decision on record
(the block has no shadow); items 2 and 7 follow from sizing each block by
its body's physical box, which is what makes the block and the click box
agree. Item 10 is the scene itself: it selects the player's own vehicle
between the two shots. The block is on screen for 0.1–0.9 s. Nothing here was
changed.
