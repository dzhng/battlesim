Supply heli: the map

Every decision for adding a landing supply helicopter, as walked on 2026-11-17. Source notes: throwaway/supply-heli/scan.md. Nothing is built yet.

Quadrant 1Known knowns

The request. One supply helicopter per faction. It costs the same as the supply truck (150), carries half its stock (500) and sits in the LOG tab. Deploying lands it and stops the rotor. Packing spins the rotor up and it flies again.

  • Trucks: HEMTT / MAN HX / Ural, cost 150, stock 1000, deploy 3 s, pack 1 s. fixtures/units/roster/{us,europe,eastern}.json
  • The shop tab comes from category + role: sup + logistics → LOG. web/src/battle/present/purchaseFilter.ts:20-37
  • Supply works whether or not the supplier flies. Range is 80 m measured flat on the map, and the source must be deployed and must not have moved. crates/sim/src/supply.rs:173-235
  • Deploy is generic: it happens automatically when idle, or by SetDeployment, and a unit can't move until packed. That gate already holds aircraft. deployment.rs:74-81, movement/air.rs:58
  • Today helis never land. Their lowest point is the low hover (tallest ground hull + 2 m). air.rs:191
  • Every roster heli has separate rotor nodes. blender/aircraft_parts.py:410
  • Base rows: CH-47F (US), Chinook HC6 (Europe), Mi-8AMTSh (Eastern). They keep their door guns and drop their seats.
