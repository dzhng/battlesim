1 · Known knowns

The request

A player-facing Armory, opened from a main-menu tile. It covers every playable unit of the three factions, grouped by type. Each unit shows cost, stats and weapons with weapon stats: everything the info card shows, and more. The unit appears in 3D with a camera you can orbit and zoom, standing in a field with armory buildings around it, in the style of WARNO and Broken Arrow. It has idle life: engine sound, animation, rotors.

What the code already pins down

  • Roster: fixtures/catalog.json holds 152 cards. 139 have unit types; the other 13 are planned drones with no type and no runtime art. There are three factions: us, europe, eastern. Seven cards are shared between factions. The browser reads the catalog with no battle running, through web/src/battle/catalog/sets.ts, which needs the game wasm.
  • Grouping data: roster.category has six values (rec inf veh sup hel air). It is a game rule: it decides who can hold objectives (crates/sim/src/objectives.rs:222) and what the AI buys. There are 9 roles. All 25 jets have role recon.
  • The info card is thin: name, strength pips, one row per weapon with ammo, and state rows (web/src/battle/present/panelRows.ts, infoPanel.tsx). Armour, speed, sensors, range, penetration and reload are shown nowhere in the game today.
  • The menu: apps/battle-lab/src/MainMenu.tsx. Pages are React state (PAGES, :145). The backdrop is a live capture battle (MenuBackdrop.tsx). PageVisit remounts on every navigation (navigation.tsx:7).
  • The renderer: Rust wgpu compiled to wasm, WebGPU only, with one shared device (appResources.ts). Camera primitives live in packages/renderer-core (CameraController).
  • Aircraft models stand on their modelled gear or skids at z = 0, as validation requires (aircraft_parts.py:15, validate.ts:812). In the sim, rotors always hover and jets never stop (crates/sim/README.md:192).
  • Squads at rest use a seeded random scatter over a disc sized to the squad (crates/sim/src/arrangement.rs:1-5, 53-63). There is no formation.
  • Default weapon (default in contract/src/weapons.rs:42) does three jobs. It is the soldier's fallback gun (preferred_mount; vehicles skip this, weapons/tick.rs:18). It is the jet's strafing gun (air/pass.rs:37). Together with unlimited ammo it sets fires_regardless, the cinematic plinking (weapons.rs:286-291). A useful shot counts reserve ammo, not the loaded round (has_rounds, :128), so a launcher is never starved.
  • Destructible props already exist through destroyed.into chains (garage_row → rubble). Garrisons and building-scaled integrity require a building template (contract/src/catalog/props.rs:170).
  • Open-country homesteads (open_country/mod.rs:457 stead) place groups of yards at road stations. The halves are even by construction (:31). The pass places prop bodies with the catalog available.
  • The native client is a replay shell with no menu (native/client/README.md:1-5). The Armory is browser-only.

3 · Unknown knowns → what was extracted

Taste: WARNO and Broken Arrow. The unit stands in a lived-in military yard, not on a void or a turntable. The bar is a shipped game's armory.
Consistency is the rule: a better pattern is adopted everywhere (all menu pages become routes; the picker shares the 9 groups), never as a one-off.
Squads: the number of men is a first-class stat because it decides carriers and buildings. Squads stand the way the game scatters them, not in parade lines.
Weapons: plinking is intentional and cinematic. What a player needs is "fires at anything" and reach per band, not engine class lists.
Consumers: players only. Browser only. Reached from the main menu only. No compare mode.
Reshaped earlier decisions: D1 (scatter, not a line), Q1′ (presentation owns idle life), Q7 → L3 (nets over supplies), U7 (the icon marks "fires regardless", not default).
