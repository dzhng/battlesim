The source of truth is specs/done/water-and-wetlands/ (PR #15): decisions.md and the README's principles and invariants. This page summarises them.

1 · Known knowns

  • Rivers block every ground unit. There is no fording; bridges are the only crossing (crates/sim/src/world/mod.rs ground_surface_at).
  • Speed is one formula: Mobility::speed, built from road, forest and slope, with craters applied after (navigation.rs). The route planner costs slow ground per tile in SlowGround.
  • Water is shaded in the main world pass with read-only depth and no copy of the scene colour. Its transparency comes from distance to the river's edge (worldPass.ts, terrainMaterial.ts).
  • Native runs the browser's exported shaders and reads the export strictly (crates/pipelines, native/client).
  • There are two winds: grass at 35° and smoke at about 27°. Trees don't sway.
  • Dust is emitted only for hulls the side can see (effects/frame.rs note_movers), which is the model for wakes.
  • Sight is a ray over the terrain from the eye (1.6 m for infantry, the hull's eye_m for vehicles) to a point 1 m up, or to the target's body (visibility.rs, sensing.rs). No swamp logic is needed.
  • We are not adopting RedGPU. We port its MIT-licensed techniques, pinned to tag 95aaf9a.

3 · Unknown knowns what got drawn out

  • "Same quality as RedGPU" means their techniques in our palette. You picked temperate and murky water and rejected the tropical look. That reshaped decision 9.
  • Several picks mean variants on the map. That became the variant rule (12, 27, 28).
  • You like emergent sight from terrain, and you rank how swamps look above balancing them. That revised decisions 6 and 21, and gave 30.
  • Simplify where gameplay doesn't need it. Puddles became look only (29), and the speed numbers dropped from six to three.
  • Native must keep running, and parity is best-effort, with a follow-up spec for the overflow. That revised decision 19.
  • Who it's for: play at close and tactical zoom, plus low cinematic angles (17). Far zoom only has to not look wrong.

4 · Unknown unknowns trap cards, worst first

Green: decided. Amber: a sharp edge the slice must handle. Red: open.

Native refuses the whole export on any value it doesn't know.

Evidence: native/client/src/pipelines.rs uses deny_unknown_fields. Its tests build pipelines but never draw.

Changes: each step teaches native the new values, and a native draw check runs every step (decision 19).

The world pass discards its MSAA samples, and there's no depth resolve.

Evidence: worldPass.ts uses storeOp: "discard" (commit 54bc94c03), and lit has no COPY_SRC usage.

Changes: slice 02 adds the copy seam. Resolve into lit, because Metal skips empty tiles and a new target would keep stale pixels.

Pass order is constrained.

The order must be opaque → copy → water → glass → effects. Glass currently draws after water and writes no depth.

Refraction samples colour before fog is applied.

This shows a seam at the fog edge. Copy the fog mask along with the colour.

Ground marks would show twice under water, once on the bed and once on the water. One layer must own the paint.
The Low tier must be a uniform.

The export never varies quality, and the look data is spliced into the shader text, so it must move to uniforms.

The river scene's checks will fail.

A shore taken from depth follows the 4 m triangles. Re-judge those checks rather than loosening them.

The dip can silently not exist.

Evidence: Carve does nothing on a map with no river, and navigation only reads registered regions.

Swamps must not become a SurfaceKind.

They need their own map list, plus a field on the sim's Surface and on the navigation Cell.

There are five speed read sites.

Use one combined SlowGround channel per mover. dense.rs has its own copy of speed().

Admission reads the land before carving.

New rules must keep swamps off buildings and bridge approaches, with a gap from river edges.

Every hash and fixture moves.

Use skip_serializing_if on the new map field, re-record the parity fixtures, bump the mapgen versions, and draw swamps from their own random stream.

Rider wakes.

A squad inside an APC stands at the APC's position, so it would leave rings. Skip units that are being carried.

The wake decision can't see feet or water yet.

Add member positions and a water lookup to the mover record. Decide wakes per tick, not from the frame-rate-dependent gait.

The wave state lives outside presentation's resets.

Presentation publishes the step count and a reset counter, and the waves clear on a side switch (decision 24). Captures warm up for a fixed number of steps.

Unifying the wind re-records test data.

It changes grass-vectors, effect-vectors and exact-numbers.json (compared as text).

The reel has no water, so the tier check never pays for it.

The capture scene now holds the targets (decision 31). The tier check stays blind until a reel shot shows water.
