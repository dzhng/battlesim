# Deferred hills and ridges

Hills and ridges are deferred work within city-maps. They are not part of its current implementation or completion gates. Broadly flat ground still needs interesting physical landmarks everywhere; riverbeds and banks remain in the current river contract.

Before starting relief, establish its landforms, tactical effects and cost with explore-unknowns. The simulation, navigation, projectile flight, rendering and picking must consume the same physical terrain. Roads, settlements and foundations must fit it while preserving usable plains and accessible approaches. Camera clearance and scale budgets must include the resulting terrain and rendered surroundings.

No height range, noise algorithm, sampling resolution, storage representation or implementation ladder is selected here.
