# Scale decisions

These 23 scale-owned decisions preserve parent ownership of generation, physical
materialization, movement admission and appearance. They do not ratify a budget.
Results and identified admission scope live in [evidence](evidence.md).

Read the medium-confidence tradeoffs first. Each is accepted only within the
reach stated here; measurement results live with identified evidence, not choices.

## Medium confidence: workload, compatibility and representation tradeoffs

### 1. Establish contact without assuming a refused journey succeeds

A script can leave rifle squads outside weapon reach when incoming movement
admission refuses their travel. Move the synthetic fronts inward through existing
legal-placement queries, retaining the complete world, scripts, roster and late
remains. This intentionally changes the scenario rather than disguising inactivity
as a cost gain. Check actual rising own-unit shot counters during measured browser
arms; ammunition loss can instead be a casualty. This proves participation, not
both forces' tactical quality or successful travel. Confidence is medium for this
synthetic fight's representativeness; refused movement stays visible.

### 2. Share one synthetic full-world workload

Native and browser stress must not quietly place different fights. One
simulation-owned contact factory supplies both, and existing preparation performs
map resolution off the page thread. The central fight retains the surrounding
full world; a separate crossing measures transit. Neither replaces assault
playability. This is a measurement choice, not a new runtime map format.

### 3. Refuse replay on a different executable simulation

The same rules and map can behave differently after a code change. Require the
build-time source/dependency/compiler fingerprint before playback, normalizing
supported target/profile differences rather than hashing binary files. This
conservatively refuses some harmless edits but avoids manual version bumps or
silent old-engine compatibility. Generated runtime inputs must stay within that
owner's coverage. Compiled-scenario replay storage remains parent debt.

### 4. Reuse exact content rather than invent transport identities

Inserting a corpse or route point shifts later words. Match exact old rows or
word spans and send literals for the rest, first choosing the smallest ordinary form, then compacting that winner if smaller.
This does not choose a global minimum across every compressed form.
Keep one immutable previous baseline and a bounded temporary matching index.
The variable-word index uses shorter exact three-word anchors through one constant
shared by construction and lookup, recovering spans a real late burst would
otherwise resend. Fixed-row strides and wire grammar stay unchanged. Accept the
larger conservative aggregate index bound (21⅓ MiB, 22,369,620 B) and measured
extra encoding work instead of adding another selector or entity schema. Greedy
matching can make individual records larger; no monotonic reduction, global
compressed minimum or whole-step gain is promised. Actual peak overlap remains
an admission obligation rather than the requested-index bound.
The CPU and transient-memory tradeoff is accepted for lossless delivery; soldier
IDs cannot replace canonical content equality or become transport-owned identities.

### 5. Certify sampled infantry corridors from living members

A connected coarse cell may contain an L-shaped opening that a straight link
cuts across. Reconstruct through its existing free mask and certify emitted
segments. Select a living start admitted by side-known navigation before the
ordinary nearest-member fallback; a centroid can lie inside a body. Fixed local
queues and incremental work avoid a world-sized fine grid. This intentionally
changes affected routes and planning state; sampled legality is not exact body
clearance. Confidence across all generated geometry remains narrower than the
focused physical examples.

### 6. Report every request without declaring long travel a failure

A distant attacker can legitimately still be marching at the end of a short
sample. Stream exact request identities, refusals, remaining distance and pending
work from the real generator/planner/Battle. Do not replace refused seeds or infer
arrival from proximity. The tooling makes faults diagnosable; its short duration
and panic reporting do not prove playability or recover a hung process.

## High confidence: shared owners and necessary-work proofs

### 7. Observe production costs outside authoritative state

The report brackets the same production tick ordinary play uses, with separate
learning, observation-construction and packing owners. Counters and profiling
overhead never become digest or replay state. A second simulation loop could drift.

### 8. Exhaust a corridor before falling back

Sampling beyond a blocked route endpoint repeats the endpoint forever. Stop once
all remaining candidates are exhausted, preserving successful order and the
existing fallback. An arbitrary retry cap could hide an untried legal candidate.

### 9. Give derived bounds the lifetime of their positions

Several vehicles need the same squad radius. Derive it in existing crowd data
and refresh after every relevant motion; sensing derives its bounds while its
input slice is immutable. Retain unit order and body behavior rather than adding
a persistent cross-tick geometry cache.

### 10. Reject only proven-empty cover searches

A target outside weapon reach plus permitted holding and leaning reach cannot
be engaged from any candidate. Skip that search using a conservative necessary
bound; near and boundary targets retain the ordinary solver. No named unit or
arbitrary distance exception is introduced.

### 11. Put fog dirty history in the existing world footprint index

A distant falling tree need not rebuild every active raster. World buckets
retain mutation stamps; moves touch old and new footprints and deletions leave
history. Tiles compare the same conservative query range after global change.
Side-known indices need no such stamps. This adds extent-bounded metadata,
not changed-prefix history or another permanent fine world grid.

### 12. Canonicalize fog candidates once at their union owner

Nearby eyes repeatedly collect the same bodies. Append raw bucket entries and
sort/deduplicate the complete union before knowledge reads; ordinary query callers
retain sorted unique output. No query cache persists. Temporary duplicates can
increase capacity, so combined Fog and Learning—not one shifted bracket—owns
the gain and memory disclosure.

### 13. Inline the existing measured foliage query

Expose the tiny existing query to its consuming compiler without changing lookup,
masking or arithmetic. This is a measured compiler/consumer decision, not a
universal target claim or a reason to duplicate visibility logic.

### 14. Reuse a strict failed-goal certificate narrowly

Many road exits can retry the same blocked final approach. Retain an exhausted
reverse component for that strict connector, and reject only exact sources
outside it. A conservative expanded whole-arc envelope may prune road candidates
before nearest access selection; ambiguous overlap falls back. Search limits
prove nothing. Rare sparse scratch and its incremental membership fingerprint
belong to the job and digest, with existing revision recovery. This changes
affected planning progress, not physical admission.

### 15. Commit delivery only after a complete record validates

A shrinking squad group must not move other groups' addresses or overwrite its
previous copy sources. Reconstruct into new storage from one immutable baseline,
preflight complete sizes and commit producer/decoder cursors only on success.
A malformed generation can retry; epochs and side changes start snapshots.
Independent buffer bounds do not constitute peak-memory admission.

### 16. Pack the selected lossless form without another selector

After selecting the ordinary winning group form, pack its small integers and literal raw float32
words when smaller. Read packed carrier words through unsigned aliases, and preserve their raw
bits through the worker's Float32Array same-type `.set` copy. This does not globally compare compressed candidates. Retain one selector, baseline
and layout grammar; introduce
no byte staging buffer, compression dependency or alternate precision.

### 17. Preserve unchanged identity through existing readers

A moving tank does not require rebuilding an unchanged corpse list. Reuse a nonempty immutable decoded group after a zero-payload replacement,
its existing feed conversion and the established pose
gate by array identity. Empty snapshot views are recreated. Changed data rebuilds;
expiry, fading, death and reset
still execute. Side/catalog/epoch lifetimes remain with existing owners instead
of a second per-row cache.

### 18. Compare reset resources at equivalent presented states

A late overlay may legitimately disappear on reset. Compare fresh resets only
after lazy resources warm and the same paused tick is presented, including bytes
as well as counts. Death-animation starts depend on earlier drawn frames; present
each paused opening tick so one final clock does not conceal different animation
ages. A fresh authority must acknowledge its first paused step; inherited pause
status and a cleared observation cannot certify the new client. Await asynchronous
work before inspecting completed settlement. The external reset timing seam
controls frame delivery while native page/worker clocks and production poses
retain their owners. A retained leak still accumulates. This fixes the measurement
state, not renderer capacity or the leak allowance.

### 19. Pack exact learned-ground runs in the existing carrier

A run's zero marks need no transmitted bytes. Encode its canonical address,
start, length and nonzero marks through the shared raw carrier, restoring all
implicit zeros. The published layout is the sole grammar; run admission and
combined logical bounds precede allocation, and whole-record validation precedes
cursor commit. No new ground baseline, staging buffer or knowledge delay results.

### 20. Return changed shared edges to the planner's revision owner

A body can close an edge after coarse admission but before infantry refinement.
Return the ordinary candidate failure rather than panicking or granting a stale
crossing. The existing changed-revision owner starts the fresh journey; refinement
adds no retry policy. This is an intentional failure-path correction.

### 21. Omit local geometry only when no member can steer

A distant moving vehicle makes global threats nonempty, although an idle squad
has neither an unfinished post nor a predicted dodge. Share holding-post
eligibility with ordinary steering and omit only that unreachable geometry work.
Retain velocity reset, garrison ordering and ordinary centroid settlement. Nearby
traffic and corridor motion still use the existing movement owner.

### 22. Omit unread work through the existing consumer proof

The sole cover-position consumer first needs an enemy track. While both knowledge
references are immutable, an ID tracked by neither side may retain an empty row
at its original unit slot. Tracked rows keep all members, including remembered
fallen members. Dead or currently unseen alone is not sufficient, and a future
untracked reader must revisit this proof.

Hull gathering has a separate necessary predicate: a unit without a physical
hull cannot emit vehicle geometry. Check that predicate before scanning members
for liveness. Vehicles keep their health check, ordered geometry and pure mapping
through the existing query; no retained hull projection or class exception is
needed. The source fingerprint changes, while battle outcomes and serialized
replay content remain exact apart from engine identity.

### 23. Use one portable evaluator for proven authoritative families

Platform math can change a seeded position, cover bearing, ricochet velocity or
rotated wall normal by one bit. A later blast proved equal local operands could
still produce a different hypot distance and retained damage. Use existing
pinned libm throughout authoritative sine, cosine, combined sincos, atan2 and
hypot, including shared physical contracts, rather than leave evaluator islands
within families already shown to diverge. Preserve each existing owner's
arguments, arithmetic order, random draws and policy; cached Rotation keeps its
combined pair. Native last bits intentionally change. No new wrapper, dependency,
tolerance, ABI or gameplay rule results.

Public seeded/warm-facing records, fixed-input ricochet decisions, Native and
actual Wasm wall-ray queries and rectangle distance pin the concrete causes.
Equal state or float32 views alone cannot establish equal transient geometry.
The scope follows captured equal impact/blast operands and first differing
operations, not a speculative inventory of all numerical functions. Other
families and parent materialization choices keep their owners. This is a
precision correction, with no performance or full-admission claim.
