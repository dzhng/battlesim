# City-map scale rationale

**Scale component admission is green on the identified layout-9 workload.
Parent generation and presentation work retain their separate scope.**
The scale lane makes a complete physical city battle cheaper without hiding late
knowledge, destruction and remains. Exact cost changes, physical routing repairs,
portable numerical corrections and benchmark-input corrections are distinct:
a quiet battle or weakened rule must not masquerade as an optimization.

One simulation-owned synthetic contact factory feeds native reports and browser
preparation. It retains the complete world around a central fight. The named
inward-front correction establishes real firing even when incoming movement
admission refuses scripted travel. It changes workload identities, not destination
truthfulness. Separate crossing and generated assault proofs retain their own
questions; dense contact does not certify tactical placement or arrival.

The [decision ledger](choices.md) holds the reasons and reach of the choices.
[Identified evidence](evidence.md) owns frozen layout-9 Native/Wasm, decoder and
requested-memory results, the integrated hull correction's bounded proof, fresh
browser throughput and distinct memory measurements, and corrected-reset
positive and negative proofs. Historical numeric controls remain canonical in
parent C05/C06/C07, with their original identities.
No old control is relabelled as a current input after catalogue or rule changes.

## Contracts that must survive

The accepted floor is **25 Hz measured throughput** early and late on a full
10 × 10 km generated reference with 100 living units a side at the start. The
nominal 30 Hz timestep and game speed are unchanged. Reaching 30 Hz and having no
tick over 33 ms remain deferred targets. The complete active-record limit is
19,800 B; cold snapshots have a separate scope. The 4 GiB guard measures requested
native heap, and the codec's 64 MiB refusal ceiling is not a performance budget.

- The simulation is the only authority. Knowledge, geometry and delivery contain
  only the evidence each side is entitled to receive. Candidate collection may
  be batched, but canonical body ordering precedes knowledge reads.
- Exact cost changes preserve authoritative digests, replayed outcomes and complete
  observations. Replay engine identities change with simulation source, including
  digest-neutral edits. Physical routing and pinned-math corrections explicitly
  name their changed state; equal float32 publications alone cannot prove state parity.
- Local invalidation follows the existing footprint-index owner, including old
  and new footprints and deletion history. It does not depend on capped change
  lists or a second full-world fine grid.
- Omitted work has no reachable consumer: a no-steer squad still settles through
  the ordinary owner, and an unread cover row is omitted only while both knowledge
  references are immutable. Dead or unseen status is not a substitute predicate.
  Hull gathering rejects non-hull units before member liveness; vehicles retain
  their health check, geometry and order through the existing pure query.
- Navigation's sampled admission remains distinct from exact body movement.
  Connector work is incremental; exhaustion can certify a component, a work limit
  cannot. A changed edge returns failure to the planner's revision owner instead
  of inventing an escape or a second retry policy.
- Publication reconstructs the exact canonical observation. Source copies read
  an immutable prior baseline; raw float bits survive NaN-shaped carriers. A
  complete malformed record commits neither side of the cursor and can retry.
  Snapshot, side, epoch and reset lifetimes remain explicit.
- Storage and active work have separate owners and proofs. A codec refusal ceiling
  is not a memory allowance. Capacity, decoded arrays, snapshots and overlapping
  worker/page copies count toward actual peak memory.
- Reset resources compare equivalent presented battle states. Lazily materialized
  late overlays may legitimately disappear; a repeated retained allocation must
  still fail. Short reset checks establish lifetime, not battle throughput.
- Reports measure the production path outside authoritative state and use
  instructions for cost comparisons. Browser rates measure actual execution;
  throughput cannot be inferred from instruction counters or a village benchmark.

## Dead ends worth retaining

Fine-route boolean walkability simplification did not establish a measured gain.
Height-query inlining grew linked code for a smaller benefit than foliage-only
inlining and was not retained. Footprint Rotation reuse preserved outputs but
missed its declared whole-Fog-plus-Learning improvement threshold; the native
compiler already hoisted trigonometry inside each sample row. No trig cache or
duplicate kernel resulted.

A retained corpse-sort projection would need several mutation owners. An unstable
sort cannot use soldier ID as construction order: reinforcement appends a new
member within an earlier unit. No speculative cache or alternate tie order ships.
A relaxed reverse reachability probe could not prove strict final-connector
failure; the final certificate is scoped to that connector, with conservative
road filtering and ambiguity fallback. Work exhaustion remains inconclusive.

Increasing movement validation allowances, bypassing demonstrated travel,
shortening scripts or treating missing goals as successful movement was rejected.
Exact cost shortcuts can reduce the price of a refusal without repairing it.

## Limits that remain with the parent

The historical matrix retains Mixed Medium seed 9's approach-balance refusal,
with no replacement seed. Since the 4 km Small tier (`ee24bfea`), Metro Medium
seed 13 is refused for town balance: its city, centred on the midline, grows
1.91 km² north of it and 0.46 km² south. The river test skips a seed refused
without a river (at most two of its 360) rather than blaming the river. Frozen vehicle obstruction proves finite refusal, not
reachable encounter placement. Fresh refused infantry extensions are idle before
planning, so they cannot prove that the ten historically pending generated
journeys now complete. Current public corner/centroid arrival and replay tests
use ordinary admission and pin their focused repairs; they do not replace that
separate generated journey finding.

No uploaded baseline or inspiration image defined this lane's appearance.
Captured browser screenshots are historical diagnostics, not design references.
Readability findings remain with renderer/UI owners. Parent city-maps generation,
appearance, startup and city frame/tour requirements remain active; the short
village benchmark supplies only an affected regression control.
