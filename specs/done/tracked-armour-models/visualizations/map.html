1 · Known knowns

  • Each family is a procedural Blender script that calls vehicle_export.run with shared parts.
  • The battle frame is gameplay: hull box, mount pivot and muzzle, and eye. Changing it changes digests.
  • Every tracked model is under its triangle cap, the largest at 61% (T-80BVM, 73k of 120k).
  • The catalog-load limit is a loose tripwire, not a target. 635 of 640 MiB is used, and half of it is wrecks.
  • Gun spread is statistical, so a shorter hull changes the hit rate only slightly.
  • The renderer already does sun highlights and sky reflections. Jet paint is matte on purpose.

3 · Unknown knowns: your taste

  • Proportions and silhouette first; this is cinematic realism, not a museum.
  • Clean factory paint, with one clean scheme per nation.
  • Crisp plates, edges that catch the light, and seams painted on.
  • Track sag between the return rollers. Hatches closed, no crew.
  • The closest look is the zoomed battle camera and cinematics.
  • From your notes, three patterns repeat across families: side panels that jut forward (11 vehicles), howitzer turrets out of scale with the hull (4), and the BMP/BRM noses (2).

  • All 25 receipts are stale.

  • 15 families were never linted.
  • Several sets of pictures must be regenerated.
  • Worktrees conflict when two of them bake.
  • Edge wear differs from lane to lane.
