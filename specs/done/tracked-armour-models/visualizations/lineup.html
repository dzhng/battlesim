Tracked armour: the lineup after the pass

All 37 tracked models on their Gemini Argon bodies, after slice 30's re-export (matte paint, hardware in a darker shade of each scheme, the swept turret fronts). Each card: the reference photo, the model before the pass and after it (the same Blender close-up), and the game renderer's battle camera at a fixed distance, with a 1.8 m figure for scale (the magenta box is the unit's hit box).
