# Slice 03 critique: the unprimed round

One fresh subagent was given only `reference-full.jpg`, the reference turret
crop, and the three candidates' 24 shots. The candidates were relabelled A,
B and C in a shuffled order, and no history came with them. It was asked to
judge the turret, especially its cast body, against the reference. This
file condenses its report. `receipts.mjs` passed it: it states no measured
quantities and names no files.

The letters were A = smoothed, B = skin, C = current.

| Candidate | Silhouette | Surface | Fittings | Materials | Cohesion | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| smoothed (A) | 5 | 7 | 5 | 5 | 6 | 1 |
| current (C) | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 2 |
| skin (B) | 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 |

**smoothed (A).**
- Clean and smooth in every matcap, with no faceting or lumps, and the
  ERA (explosive reactive armour bricks) sits firmly on it.
- Its fault is shape: it reads as a soft, low bowl. The reference has
  steeper lower walls and a hard wedge front.
- Fixes:
  - steepen the lower wall and sharpen the front wedge (medium confidence);
  - left-flank bricks hang like loose blocks (medium).

**current (C).**
- Its proportions are close to A's. The hard crease between a vertical
  lower band and the sloped roof hints at the real shape.
- It is visibly low-poly: flat facets catch the light unevenly, and one
  bright facet sits beside darker ones.
- Fixes:
  - add segments or smooth the normals (high confidence);
  - keep the lower band and its crease into the roof, but round the crease
    slightly (medium).

**skin (B).**
- Too tall and round, "like a balloon".
- Its lower skirt is crumpled like cloth, with jagged, spiky shading in the
  cinematic matcap.
- The front ERA has sunk into the dome until only stubs show.
- Fixes:
  - remove the crumpled noise (high confidence);
  - deflate the dome (high);
  - raise the bricks out of the body (high);
  - cut back the plastic-looking highlight (medium).

**Shared by all three, outside this slice's variable:**
- The smoke launchers sit front-centre and read like headlights.
- The front ERA fans out like petals instead of a flat, forward-jutting
  chevron.
- The mantlet is a box, not a bulged fabric cover.
- The paint is flat.
