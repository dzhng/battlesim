Control groups and range-select · the map

The bottom unit bar gets Shift-click range-select and control groups on Ctrl+0–9. The game shows grouped cards as white brackets on the left of the bar. This page records everything settled during the walk, who settled it, and what a builder must still confirm.

Q1 · Known knowns settled ground

  • The bar is ArmyDeck in web/src/battle/present/armyDeck.tsx. It only shows cards: selected and onSelect come from outside. Cards are 64 px wide with a 4 px gap, and the bar wraps into balanced rows using a 68 px-per-card capacity calculation (line 74).
  • Card order follows the order the observation lists your own units, filtered to units you control (controlled.ts, BattleView.tsx:179). Waiting reinforcements come last, as disabled cards.
  • Selection belongs to useUnitControl. It drops dead units and riders however they got in, resets on a new client (restart), and fires onSelect for added units, which triggers spoken answers.
  • Keys are listed in CommandBindings and matched by physical key code. The digits are free. isGameKey (keyGuard.ts:26) rejects every press with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held.
  • Unit ids are never reused within a battle: a new id is the unit list's length, and the list never shrinks (crates/sim/src/battle/purchase_preview.rs:78).
  • The contract: nothing changes between the simulation and the client. There's no new command, no observation field, no digest change, and replays are untouched. Control groups are client presentation and input only.

Q4 · Unknown unknowns landmines and sharp edges

OPENCan a page block Ctrl+1–9 in Windows Chrome?

Chrome uses these keys to switch tabs. From memory, they aren't on Chrome's list of shortcuts a page can never block, but that's untested. Unblock: a five-minute test on Windows early in the build.

OPENThe deep-blue digit is a new shade

The HUD's normal blue is too pale to read on white. Unblock: add a named colour to presentation.hud and check it reads at a 15 px tab. Never write the value into the code.

sharp edgeCtrl+digit needs its own path

isGameKey rejects any press with Ctrl held (keyGuard.ts:26). The group keys need a deliberate check of their own. Don't loosen the shared filter.

sharp edgeThe layout becomes simpler, not more complex

With no gaps, the existing rows and columns calculation from card count and capacity stays as it is. Changing the grid to fill by columns (grid-auto-flow: column with fixed rows) produces the order itself, and cardOffset is deleted.

sharp edgeThe double-tap needs the camera

focusUnit lives in BattleView.tsx:141. The selection hook needs a callback to reach it.

sharp edgeCovered corners

The number tab covers the empty top-left corner of a card. On hover, the × tab covers the top-right, where a riding squad's seat mark sits.

sharp edgePressing N again can be silent

If the group is already selected, no unit is added, so nobody speaks.

sharp edgeCtrl+0 only blocked during play

With the pause menu open, the browser gets Ctrl+0 back and resets the zoom.

sharp edgeAn existing test flips meaning

web/tests/armyDeck.test.tsx checks today's Shift-click add-or-remove. Write the range test first.

Addendum · Fire blink on cards added after the walk

The contract: each tick, the simulation publishes for each of your own units whether an enemy round aimed at it passed near it. Like voice lines, this stays out of the battle digest, so outcomes, digests and replays are unchanged. Every round records what it was fired at; today only fused rounds do (fused_target, battle.rs:104). Damage needs no change, because each unit's health and each soldier's health are already published every tick.
