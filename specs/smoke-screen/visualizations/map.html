Smoke screen: the four-quadrant map

Explore-unknowns walk, 2026-11-12. The spec in specs/smoke-screen/ carries every decision below verbatim.

1 · Known knowns: settled ground

  • A transient sight-blocking prop exists (catalog/props.rs:27, expiry battle.rs:1845-1879), but nothing creates one, no shipped row has a lifetime, and it blocks like a solid box. The smoke cloud replaces it.
  • Last contact already behaves as wanted: a fixed LastSeen area on the first unseen tick (knowledge.rs:213), 30 s, refreshed by every shot. General-purpose rounds (tank HE, HMG) keep shelling it; AP and guided rounds don't (weapons.rs:296, 869).
  • Rounds are physical (flight/mod.rs:1), so a unit that sits in its smoke can still be hit.
  • Flares are the charge template: capability, sim state, digest, resupply, publication, panel row (catalog.rs:253, units.rs:362, supply.rs:84, panelRows.ts:94).
  • Forest sight: reach = range × concealment × e−depth, blocked at depth 1.0 (sensing.rs:99-125). Forest ground concealment is 0.25 (rules.json:1006). In practice, forest hides a unit through that 0.25, not foliage depth.
  • Reverse is a player order (command.rs:21). Nothing reverses on its own today.

4 · Unknown unknowns: traps found by the sweep

Smoke can't be foliage cells decidedfoliage_depth returns 0 when a map has no forest, and it only measures inside forest bounds (forest.rs:302-339). Artillery and SAM launch checks refuse to fire when any foliage lies on the launch path (weapons.rs:532).
→ Smoke gets its own term, the length of the sight line inside each circle, added to foliage depth in the sight paths only. Launch checks keep reading tree crowns only.
The cloud is not a prop decidedA prop is a box, sides remember props forever (so expired clouds would linger as ghosts), it has no birth tick, and adding one forces navigation replans and makes helicopters climb over it (movement/mod.rs:206, air.rs:321).
→ Smoke clouds get their own collection in the world, with a pop tick. The old transient-prop machinery (lifetime_s, expiries, Q28 tests) is deleted, not kept alongside.
Fog caches miss a growing or fading cloud decidedThe simulation's fog cache only notices obstacle changes (visibility.rs:55-62). The GPU fog rebuilds every eye whenever foliage changes (sight.rs:876).
→ Each fog sweep is keyed on the clouds within that observer's reach. The renderer receives clouds as a list of circles and rebuilds only the eyes near a change. Fading moves in a few steps to cap rebuilds.
Publication layout changes sharp edgeAdding cloud records, a smoke-charges field and the hearing change alters a closed layout (reader.rs:170). That forces new browser capture runs (paris-corner, market-town) and new codec, fog and effects vectors (fixtures/README.md:293).
→ This is unavoidable. Plan the re-capture, and pull LFS first.
Pop must not cancel the current order decidedSettings-style orders replace the current order (battle.rs:3283), and queued orders get wiped.
→ Popping smoke is an instant state change outside the replacing path; UpgradeMove is the precedent. A unit without a charge ignores it silently. The AI pops smoke before ordering the reverse, because of the order rate limit.
The AI can't tell it's losing decidedThe AI reads no hit points, and attacker memory isn't published (mod.rs:889-935). It also re-orders an attack 5 s later, overwriting its own reverse.
→ Keep an hp baseline per unit in the doctrine (friendly fire counts, which is accepted). Skip units that are reversing toward a goal. No extra random draws, so existing skirmishes don't reshuffle.
Digests decidedCopying flares would fold a word into every unit's digest and shift every pinned digest (digest.rs:62).
→ Fold smoke state only when the unit has smoke or clouds exist. Test units get no smoke, so pinned fixtures stay put. The digest changes for roster battles are a named decision in choices.md.
Roster profiles mix in other units decidedThe light-tank profile also covers artillery, and the IFV profile also covers SAMs. Rows inherit from each other (Abrams v2 → v3 → M1E3).
→ Smoke is authored on each unit row, not on a profile or part, and inheritance is checked so each row ends with the intended charge count. The assault squad is Europe and Eastern only; US Marines fill that role.
Drawn smoke won't fit a dense cloud decidedSmoke puffs always drift downwind, share a 4096-puff budget with wreck smoke, are unsorted, and use a soft fade that may open a clear bubble around a tank (smoke.rs:13, frame.rs:655).
→ Add a new screen look with its own law that stays inside the circle and a budget kept apart from wreck smoke. Judge the bubble and the popping on screenshots.
Guided missiles sharp edgeWire- and laser-guided missiles can't launch into or out of smoke, and one in flight loses guidance for good when its operator loses sight. Fire-and-forget seekers ignore smoke, and interceptors lose rounds inside it (fire.rs:98-160).
→ This emerges from existing rules with no new work. Smoke beats wire-guided missiles; a Javelin still hits.
Squads and edges sharp edgeA squad can spread wider than the 12 m cloud, and a squad is identified once 20% of its soldiers are seen (sensing.rs:54). A helicopter looking almost straight down may see in.
→ Acceptable. These are tuning, not rules.
Resupply and refund decidedResupply serves smoke after replacement soldiers. Price and service time are authored on the capability, which keeps the frozen physical-rules file untouched. Refund value ignores smoke, as it ignores flares.
