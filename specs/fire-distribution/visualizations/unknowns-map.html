Overkill coordination

Rule. A mount firing a weapon that opts in (avoids_overkill) holds its shot while the damage its side already has flying at the target, landing before its own round would, reaches the target's remaining health. It keeps its aim on the target and fires the moment the target stops being doomed.

Built in a five-stage walk on 2026-11-19. Every decision below was shown to the user. None were delegated.

Known knownsSettled ground

  • Each mount picks its own target in select (crates/sim/src/weapons.rs:597). It ranks targets by seeker match, threat to itself, armour share, cost, distance and id. Nothing reads other mounts, so focus fire happens by default.
  • Locks are sticky: automatic_lock (weapons.rs:823) keeps a target until the mount can no longer shoot it.
  • Units act in id order and write their locks back during tick::advance (weapons/tick.rs:85). Later units see earlier units' work from the same tick.
  • Each tick runs flight with damage applied, then sensing, then fire (battle.rs:595–610). Health is current when targets are chosen, and every round still in flight is in Battle.rounds.
  • Every round in flight records the unit it was aimed at in Round.aimed_at (battle/fire.rs:16). A flare clears it (battle/countermeasures.rs:73).
  • A vehicle has a single hp. A squad's health is per soldier. Trophy charges live in Unit.protection (protection.rs).
  • Explicit attack orders win in choose_lock (weapons.rs:755). The skirmish AI focuses fire through those orders.
  • There is no prior overkill or fire-distribution code, spec or commit.
  • The only mounts that share several weapon kinds are tank cannons (AP + HE) and one IFV (ifv_he + bastion). Grenades and RPGs each sit on their own mount.

Unknown knownsWhat the walk drew out

Taste

  • Simplest mechanism that still holds up on screen. The user asked for it outright and kept cutting machinery.
  • Cinematic over efficient. A default gun plinks at armour, a crew follows up a miss instantly, a gun never idles behind a slow missile.
  • Inaccurate fire never coordinates. This shaped D2/D3 and removed the 2× margin and the derived exemptions.
  • Data over derivation. The scope is an authored flag, not a rule inferred from guidance, spread or role.
  • Internal automatic behaviour may read real state.

Scenes the rule must produce

  • S1 Three Javelins, MBT at 200 hp: 105, then 210, so the third team holds.
  • S2 Three tanks, MBT at 60 hp, same-tick aim: 45, then 90, so tank C holds.
  • S3 Two ATGMs, 45-hp light vehicle: the second holds and fires at once if the first misses.
  • S4 MBT at 50 hp with one Trophy charge: the first missile is absorbed, the second fires, a third holds.
  • S5 An autocannon on a BMP with a Javelin in flight: the autocannon is unflagged and keeps firing.
  • S6 An MG, rifles or HE on a squad: unchanged.
  • S7 A default gun on a tank, AA, artillery: unchanged.
  • S8 A missile released or flared mid-flight: the held team fires.

Rule: a mount whose chosen kind (preferred_kind) against its target is flagged holds when the damage its side has committed to that target, landing before its own shot (Ready.time_of_flight_s), reaches the target's actual remaining hp (vehicle hp, or sum of living soldiers' hp).
- Committed = the side's flagged rounds in flight with aimed_at == target, plus earlier launches this tick (running tally in tick::advance, unit-id order). Never locks.
- Round damage = damage x pierce_share on the face it meets (bounces 0). Arrival = distance / current speed.
- Trophy: the first N interceptable committed rounds by arrival count zero, N = charges left; ignore cooldown.
- A guided round stops counting once its guidance is released; flares already clear aimed_at.
- The hold is a gate at the firing step: keep the lock, aim, reload and turret tracking; fire on the first tick the target isn't doomed. Never switch ammo or weapon to escape a hold; only a target change escapes it.
- A doomed current lock triggers reconsider; select ranks doomed targets last for flagged kinds.
- A held mount still sets Reach.can_engage.
- Explicit attack orders and default-gun fire at unhurtable targets are untouched.
