# 07 — Effects, scars, and grounding

**Contract:** native renders observed effect causes from the Rust-owned publication stream without inferring gameplay or visibility.

**Seam:** extend the capture/layout manifest only if required to expose projectiles, blasts, guided events, dust, smoke, muzzle/tracer, craters, scorch, tracks, trample, selection/range grounding, and effect timing. Browser owners are `effects/effectFrame.ts`, `effects/effectPass.ts`, `scarTexture.ts`, `grassPass.ts`, and fog passes.

**Runnable checkpoint:** effect-bearing market-town and close combat cuts with deterministic replay, terrain anchoring, depth ordering, and hidden-cell suppression.

**Focused gates:** semantic cause decoding, interpolation/timing, no effect leakage through fog, no floating units/props, deterministic capture/replay, and resource cleanup. Do not infer dust from camera motion or map geometry.

**Visual variable:** dust, smoke, flashes, tracers, scars, and physical grounding. Sky/material tuning is out of scope.

**Acceptance evidence:** effect-heavy crops, `compare-screenshots`, unprimed `screenshot-critique`, and `preview-shots`.
