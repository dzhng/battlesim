Lane slots in the skirmish lobby: map of unknowns

Your slot number is your lane: it's painted on the lobby triangles and on markers in battle, and the AI leans toward its own lane. Plus three lobby cleanups. Walk finished 2026-11-14.

Superseded by the plan. This is the explore-unknowns map as delivered on 2026-11-14, kept for its reasoning. The spec README is the source of truth. Contracts first

  • New lane rule, owned by sim::entries: a side's lanes are numbered 1…n from the map's high-x end, which the lobby picture draws at the top. Slot k (the k-th player on its side) owns lane k when k ≤ n. Slots beyond n own no lane.
  • The entry list keeps its order (centre, then the outer roads; I18). Jets coming in by the centre road (D15) and ties going to the centre keep using it. Lanes are a second ordering of the same entries, never a re-sort of the list.
  • Lobby picture markup (skirmish_picture, crates/mapgen/src/inspect/lobby.rs): every entry becomes a style-D tag carrying its number and data-lane="N". The signature stays the same, and the picture still knows nothing about players.
  • No new field on the player contract. A slot's lane comes from where the player sits on their side, in the side-index ids that skirmishPlayers already assigns.
  • The client gets lane numbers from Rust, served alongside the skirmish sites. TypeScript never works them out itself.
  • AI tuning gets one new number in fixtures/skirmish-ai.json (the home-lane pull). This changes battle digests, with one named re-baseline.

Settled ground (from the territory)

  • Entries are shared by the side. A bought unit uses the side's entry with the shortest route (D8, specs/done/team-skirmish/choices.md:381). Unchanged.
  • Entry count per side is 1, 2 or 3, set by the larger side (fixtures/skirmish-sizes.json). 4v4 and 5v5 still have 3 roads.
  • The AI planner re-plans every 5 s. With up to 3 AI players, it gives each one a different lane (crates/sim/src/skirmish_ai/team/mod.rs:329). Cost is route length in metres minus bonuses (mod.rs:315). Help requests (I54) move forces between lanes separately.
  • A slot→entry rule already exists for the opening camera, in TypeScript: (N−1) mod count over the centre-first list (web/src/battle/prepare/prepare.ts:194, I17).
  • The preview restarts only on a map-input change (isMapInputChange, apps/battle-lab/src/lobby/model.ts:204). Who sits where is not a map input.
  • The lobby picture maps a world point (x, y) to (height − y, width − x) (lobby.rs:82), so Blue is on the left and the picture's top is the map's high-x end.
  • There is no in-battle entry marker today. The objectives' world-anchored HUD flags live in web/src/battle/present/objectiveMarkers.tsx.
  • Regions: Random, Taiwan, New York, Paris (fixtures/map-presets.json → REGIONS).

What surfaced along the way

  • The purpose changed mid-walk. The request started as "pick my starting location". Then you pointed out that spawning ignores it anyway. The real goal is team coordination: people owning lanes in human multiplayer. This led to D1, D6, D7 and D9.
  • Who reads the numbers: teammates in human-vs-human matches, calling lanes by number. So there are no names, and the numbers have to stay readable in the middle of a battle.
  • Taste: you went with the boldest option (D) over the subtler ones, and picked banner/triangle order over the existing camera order. Your own lane should stand out.
  • What you expect from the AI: "at the start, everyone takes their assigned lane; as the battle goes on, it goes where the need is." That sentence is now D8's acceptance test.
  • Future: the minimap will look like the lobby picture, so lane tags belong in the shared Rust picture, not in lobby-only code.

Landmines (the sweep covered the lobby, picture, camera, planner, entry rules and the tests that pin them)

Evidence: cost is in metres (mod.rs:315, distance_weight 1.0), with distinct lanes per AI (mod.rs:329), and incumbency 80 (fixtures/skirmish-ai.json:28).
